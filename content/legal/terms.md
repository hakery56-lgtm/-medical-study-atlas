## 1. What the site is

Medical Study Atlas is a study library for medical students. It collects lecture files and adds study material made from them: summaries, flash cards, practice exams, Arabic (bilingual) versions and a 3D anatomy explorer.

- Drug doses and clinical guidance in study material can be outdated or simplified. Never use them for real patients.
