## 1. E-Commerce

- ⭐ **E-Commerce (Electronic Commerce)** = **buying and selling goods and services online** using digital platforms (**websites or mobile applications**); a modern business model that **saves time and money** and reaches a **wide audience**.
- **Main components:**

| Component | Role |
|---|---|
| **Website or application** | Online platform displaying products and services |
| **Online payment system** | Secure payment by cards or digital wallets |
| **Product catalog** | Images, prices and product descriptions |
| **Customer support** | Assists users and solves their problems |
| **Delivery and logistics** | Ensures ordered products reach customers |

- **Benefits:** **global market access**, **lower operational costs**, **24/7 availability**, **easy price and product comparison**.
- **Challenges and risks:** **security and privacy concerns**, **fraud and scams**, **delivery delays or failures**, **lack of personal interaction with customers**.

**Key points**
- E-Commerce = buying/selling online via websites or apps.
- 5 components: website/app, payment system, catalog, customer support, delivery/logistics.
- Benefits: global access, lower costs, 24/7, comparison; risks: security, fraud, delivery delays, no personal contact.

</div>

## 2. Technical components of E-Commerce

| Component | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Electronic Data Interchange (EDI)** | Electronic exchange of data and business documents between two organizations | Automatically sending invoices or purchase orders between a seller and a supplier |
| **Electronic publishing** | Promoting products/services through digital marketing channels | Social media, emails, blogs, online ads |
| **Electronic messaging** | Exchanging electronic messages and documents between businesses and customers | Purchase invoices by email; delivery updates by text message |
| **Corporate digital library** | A collection of digital information available through a website or app | Product details and catalogs |

<div class="key-points">

**Key points**
- Four technical components: EDI, electronic publishing, electronic messaging, corporate digital library.
- EDI = organization-to-organization documents; publishing = marketing; messaging = business–customer messages.

</div>

**Short-essay questions**

<details><summary>Q1. What is EDI? Give an example.</summary>

**Answer:** A system for the electronic exchange of data and business documents between two organizations, e.g., automatically sending invoices or purchase orders between a seller and a supplier.

**Why:** Slide "Technical components" (EDI).
</details>

<details><summary>Q2. Differentiate electronic publishing from electronic messaging.</summary>

**Answer:** Electronic publishing promotes products or services through digital marketing channels (social media, emails, blogs, ads); electronic messaging exchanges messages and documents between businesses and customers (e.g., invoices by email, delivery updates by SMS).

**Why:** Slide "Technical components".
</details>

<details><summary>Q3. What is a corporate digital library?</summary>

**Answer:** A collection of digital information, such as product details or catalogs, available through a website or mobile app.

## 3. E-Banking

- ⭐ **E-Banking (Electronic Banking)** = a system that lets customers perform **financial transactions over the Internet or through mobile devices**; also called **online banking or virtual banking**.
- **Key features:** **fast transaction processing**, **24/7 availability**, **less need for physical bank visits**.
- **Types of E-Banking services:**

| Service | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Online banking** | Bank website: check balances, transfer funds, pay bills |
| **ATM and debit card services** | Withdraw money at ATMs or pay at POS terminals |
| **Phone banking** | Basic transactions by phone call |
| **SMS banking** | Notifications or limited operations by text message |
| **Electronic alerts** | Real-time updates on account activity |
| **Mobile banking** | Mobile apps for transfers, payments and deposits |

<div class="key-points">

**Key points**
- E-Banking = online/virtual banking via the Internet or mobile devices.
- Features: fast, 24/7, fewer branch visits.
- 6 services: online, ATM/debit, phone, SMS, alerts, mobile.

</div>

## 4. Advantages, challenges and future trends of E-Banking

| Advantages | Challenges |
|---|---|
| **Easy access to accounts anytime and anywhere** | **Cyber-attacks: hacking, phishing, identity theft** |
| **Reduced operational costs for banks and customers** | **Technical failures and service interruptions** |
| **Convenient and flexible transactions** | **Poor technological infrastructure in some regions** |
| **Enhanced security through encryption and alerts** | **Customers' lack of trust or reluctance to use digital systems** |

- ⭐ **Future trends:** **AI** (personalized and automated services), **blockchain** (secure and transparent transactions), **biometric authentication** (fingerprint, facial recognition), **advanced cybersecurity systems**.
- **Conclusion:** E-Commerce and E-Banking are **two sides of the same digital economy**: E-Commerce **enables online trade**, E-Banking provides the **secure payment infrastructure** that supports it; understanding how they work and their risks is essential for safe participation in the digital world.

**Key points**
- Advantages: access anytime, lower costs, convenience, encryption and alerts.
- Challenges: cyber-attacks, technical failures, weak infrastructure, lack of trust.
- Trends: AI, blockchain, biometrics, advanced cybersecurity.

</div>

## ⚠️ Exam traps
- **EDI = between two ORGANIZATIONS; electronic messaging = between businesses and CUSTOMERS.**
- **Electronic publishing = digital MARKETING (social media, email, blogs, ads), not document exchange.**
- **SMS banking = limited operations by text; mobile banking = app-based transfers, payments, deposits.**
- **ATM/debit services include paying at POS terminals.**
- **E-Banking is the payment infrastructure behind E-Commerce.**
- **Biometric = fingerprint and facial recognition; blockchain = secure and transparent transactions.**
