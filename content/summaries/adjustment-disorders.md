## 1. Definition and the link with physical pain

- ⭐ **Adjustment disorders** = **excessive reactions to stress** involving **negative thoughts, strong emotions and changes in behavior**; the reaction is **much more intense than typically expected**.
- Distress may appear as **anxiety, depression and behavioral disturbances**.
- **Link with physical pain:** often **intricate and complex** — pain may be a symptom of **underlying medical conditions**, while **psychological factors can exacerbate or maintain pain**.
- In an adjustment disorder, emotional distress → **heightened pain perception, reduced pain tolerance, difficulty coping with discomfort**.

### Manifestations in patients with physical pain
1. **Exaggerated pain responses**
2. **Increased anxiety about pain**
3. **Difficulty coping with pain**
4. **Increased emotional distress due to pain**
5. **Reduced physical functioning due to pain**
6. **Social withdrawal due to pain**

- Distress: anxiety, depression, behavioral disturbances.
- Pain + adjustment disorder: ↑ perception, ↓ tolerance, poor coping; six manifestations (exaggerated response, anxiety, coping difficulty, distress, reduced function, withdrawal).

## 2. Diagnosis and treatment

- **Diagnosis** needs a **careful and thorough assessment**: **rule out underlying medical conditions** contributing to pain; consider the **history of stressors, coping mechanisms and existing mental health conditions**; use **DSM-5** criteria.
- **Treatment = multidisciplinary** (psychological interventions + medical management):
  1. **Psychotherapy:** **CBT** (identify and modify negative thoughts and behaviors linked to stress and pain), **supportive therapy, psychodynamic therapy**.
  2. **Medication:** for **specific symptoms (anxiety, depression, insomnia)**; choice depends on **patient needs and severity**; **part of a comprehensive plan, often with psychotherapy**.
  3. **Pain management techniques:** **relaxation, mindfulness, physical therapy**.
  4. **Lifestyle modifications:** **regular physical activity, healthy diet, adequate sleep**.
- **Role of the psychiatrist:** **diagnosis and assessment; individual therapy; CBT; stress management techniques; support and education for patients and families; collaboration with physicians and physical therapists** for a comprehensive plan.

**Key points**
- Diagnosis: rule out medical causes, history of stressors/coping/mental health, DSM-5.
- Treatment: multidisciplinary — psychotherapy (CBT), medication for symptoms, pain-management techniques, lifestyle.
- Psychiatrist: assess, treat (CBT, individual therapy), stress management, educate, collaborate.

## 3. Prognosis and conclusion

- **Prognosis:** **generally positive with appropriate treatment**; outcome depends on **severity, individual resilience, effectiveness of treatment**.
- **Goal:** **reduce the impact of the disorder on the individual's life**, including **physical and emotional well-being**.
- With support patients **learn to manage symptoms, improve quality of life and develop healthier coping**; some need **ongoing support to prevent relapse**.
- **Conclusion:** adjustment disorders and physical pain are **interconnected** and need a **holistic approach**; recognizing the **role of psychological factors in pain** is crucial.
