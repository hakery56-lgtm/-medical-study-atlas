## 1. The swab, types and sterilization

- **Medical sterile swab:** **no living microorganisms or spores** (no bacteria, fungi, spores or viruses); **dry or moist with transport medium**; small piece of **cotton or cloth** to apply medicine, clean discharge or collect specimens.
- **Body-site types:** **nasal, eye, ear, mouth, laryngeal, vaginal, urethral, rectal, skin.**
- **Two forms:** **glass tube swab** → sterilized by **autoclave**; **disposable plastic tube swab** → **ethylene oxide gas** or **radiation (UV, gamma, X-ray)**.
- **Structure:** **wooden rod 5–10 cm** inside a test tube with **cotton on the end**; tube mouth closed with **gauze or special rubber**; ready after sterilization.

## 2. Swab collection

- **Steps:** **(1) prepare a sterile swab by autoclave → (2) insert a flexible mini-tip swab (nose, ear, mouth) → (3) gently rub and roll → (4) leave several seconds to absorb secretion → (5) remove slowly while rotating, then immediately culture, place in normal saline, or put a drop on a slide for microscopy.**
- **Pictures:** A mouth, B nose, C eye (twist cotton tip upward, patient looks downward), D ear, E skin, F vaginal/urethral.

<div class="key-points">

**Key points**
- Rub, roll, hold, rotate out.
- Culture immediately or examine at once.

</div>

## 3. Vaginal swab and trichomoniasis

- **Indications:** **vaginitis (Trichomonas vaginalis), vaginosis, uterine sepsis**; in males **prostatic and urethral secretions**.
- **Technique:** insert into the **upper vagina** and **rotate before withdrawing**.
- **Diagnosis:** **wet preparation** — mix swab material with **normal saline** on a slide and look under the light microscope for **motile trophozoites of Trichomonas vaginalis** (vaginal or cervical swab; male urethral or prostatic secretions **after prostatic massage**).

<div class="key-points">

**Key points**
- Wet mount shows motile trophozoites.
- Males: prostatic secretions after massage.

</div>
