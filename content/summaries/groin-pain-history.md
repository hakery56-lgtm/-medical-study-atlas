## 1. Presenting complaint: SOCRATES

| Letter | Item | What to ask |
|---|---|---|
| **S** | **Site** | **Unilateral or bilateral? Groin, hip or lower abdomen?** |
| **O** | **Onset** | **Sudden or gradual? Any trauma or injury?** |
| **C** | **Character** | **Sharp, dull, aching, burning or stabbing?** |
| **R** | **Radiation** | **Thigh, lower abdomen, back or genital area?** |
| **A** | **Associated symptoms** | **Lump or swelling, fever, limping, hip stiffness, nausea or vomiting, urinary, testicular or bowel symptoms** |
| **T** | **Timing** | **Constant or intermittent? Duration of each episode? Worse at night or morning?** |
| **E** | **Exacerbating / relieving** | **Walking, running, hip movement, coughing, sneezing, lifting; what relieves it?** |
| **S** | **Severity** | **Score 0–10; effect on walking, daily activities and sleep** |

<div class="key-points">

**Key points**
- SOCRATES = Site, Onset, Character, Radiation, Associated symptoms, Timing, Exacerbating/relieving, Severity.
- Site: groin, hip or lower abdomen; radiation: thigh, lower abdomen, back, genitalia.
- Severity: 0–10 plus effect on walking, daily activities and sleep.

</div>

**Short-essay questions**

## 2. Past medical and surgical history

- ⭐ **PMH:** previous episodes of groin or hip pain; **hip arthritis or joint disease**; **previous inguinal or femoral hernia**; **trauma or fractures**; **diabetes mellitus or inflammatory arthritis**; previous **urinary or genital infections.**
- ⭐ **PSH:** previous **hip or pelvic surgery**; **hernia repair**; **abdominal or urological surgery**; **postoperative complications.**

<div class="key-points">

**Key points**
- PMH: prior pain episodes, joint disease, hernia, trauma/fractures, diabetes/inflammatory arthritis, urinary/genital infections.
- PSH: hip/pelvic surgery, hernia repair, abdominal/urological surgery, complications.

</div>

**Short-essay questions**

**Answer:** Previous episodes of groin or hip pain, hip arthritis or joint disease, previous inguinal or femoral hernia, trauma or fractures, diabetes mellitus or inflammatory arthritis, and previous urinary or genital infections.

**Why:** Slide 3.
</details>

## 3. Drug, family and social history

- ⭐ **Drug history:** current medications; **analgesics and anti-inflammatory drugs**; **corticosteroids**; **anticoagulants**; **drug allergies.**
- ⭐ **Family history:** **osteoarthritis or rheumatoid arthritis**; **hip disorders**; **inguinal or femoral hernia**; **inherited musculoskeletal disorders.**
- ⭐ **Social history:** **occupation and nature of work**; **physical activity and sports**; **smoking and alcohol**; **ability to walk and perform daily activities**; **effect of pain on work and daily life**; **use of walking aids.**

<div class="key-points">

**Key points**
- Drugs: analgesics/NSAIDs, corticosteroids, anticoagulants, allergies.
- Family: arthritis, hip disorders, hernia, inherited MSK disorders.
- Social: work, sports, smoking/alcohol, daily function, walking aids.

</div>

## ⚠️ Exam traps
- **S-O-C-R-A-T-E-S: the first S = Site, the last S = Severity.**
- **Hernia appears in PMH, PSH AND family history.**
- **Anticoagulants and corticosteroids belong to the drug history.**
- **Walking aids and smoking/alcohol belong to the social history.**
- **Severity is a 0–10 score plus effect on walking, daily activities and sleep.**
