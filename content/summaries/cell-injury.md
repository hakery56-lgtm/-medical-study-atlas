## 1. شنو يعني Pathology؟ ومنين تبدي القصة

الـ **Pathology** حرفياً يعني دراسة (**logos**) المعاناة (**pathos**). يعني نعرف ليش الإنسان يمرض وشلون.

أي مرض ندرسه من أربع زوايا، وهاي هي "core of pathology":

1. **Cause (etiology)** — شنو السبب؟
2. **Mechanisms of development (pathogenesis)** — شلون تطور المرض خطوة بخطوة؟
3. **Morphologic changes** — شنو التغيرات اللي تصير بالخلايا والأعضاء (structural alterations)؟
4. **Clinical significance** — شنو النتيجة الوظيفية لهاي التغيرات، يعني شنو يحس المريض؟

هسه القصة الأساسية للملزمة:

- خلايا جسمنا طول الوقت تعدّل structure و function مالتها حتى تتماشى مع التغيرات بالـ demand والـ stress. هاي حالة التوازن نسميها **homeostasis**.
- إذا الخلية واجهت **stress** — سواء **physiological** لو **pathological** — تسوي **adaptation** حتى تحافظ على حياتها (viability) ووظيفتها وتوصل لـ **new steady state**.
- إذا الـ adaptive capacity انتهت، أو الأذى كان **severe and prolonged** → يصير **cell injury**.
- الـ cell injury ممكن يكون **reversible** (ضمن حدود معينة ترجع الخلية لـ baseline) أو **irreversible** → **cell death**.

يعني تخيلها سلّم: normal cell → stress → adaptation → إذا ما كفّت → reversible injury → إذا استمر → irreversible injury → death.

<div dir="ltr" class="key-points">

**Key points**
- Pathology = study (logos) of suffering (pathos).
- 4 aspects: etiology, pathogenesis, morphologic changes, clinical significance.
- Homeostasis = steady state; stress → adaptation → if exceeded → cell injury (reversible or irreversible → cell death).

</div>

**أسئلة Short essay**

<details><summary>Q1. List the four aspects of a disease process that form the core of pathology.</summary>

**Answer:** 1) Cause (etiology). 2) Mechanism of development (pathogenesis). 3) Structural alterations in cells and organs (morphologic changes). 4) Functional consequences of these changes (clinical significance).

**ليش؟** هاي الأربعة حرفياً مكتوبة بالملزمة وتجي سؤال تعداد مباشر، احفظها بالترتيب.
</details>

<details><summary>Q2. Define homeostasis and explain what happens when a cell faces stress.</summary>

**Answer:** Homeostasis is the steady state of equilibrium in which cells continuously adjust their structure and function to accommodate changes in demand and external stress. Under physiological or pathological stress the cell undergoes adaptation to preserve its viability and function and reach a new steady state; if the adaptive capacity is exceeded or the injury is severe and prolonged, cell injury results.

**ليش؟** السؤال يريد التسلسل كامل: homeostasis → adaptation → injury.
</details>

<details><summary>Q3. What are the possible outcomes of cell injury?</summary>

**Answer:** Cell injury may be reversible within certain limits (the cell returns to its stable baseline state) or irreversible, leading to death of the affected cells.

**ليش؟** الفرق الأساسي بين النوعين هو هل الخلية ترجع لطبيعتها لو لا.
</details>

## 2. Reversible vs Irreversible cell injury

هنا الملزمة تقسم الـ cell injury لقسمين:

1. **Reversible cell injury** — التغيرات بالخلية **تختفي** إذا شلنا الـ **injurious agent**، والخلية ترجع **normal function and morphology**.
2. **Irreversible cell injury** — إذا الـ injurious agent **بقى مستمر (persist)** أو كان **sudden and severe**، التغيرات ما ترجع وتتقدم لـ **cell death**.

الفكرة ببساطة: المفتاح هو "هل شلنا السبب بالوقت المناسب؟" و"شكد قوي السبب؟".

<div dir="ltr" class="key-points">

**Key points**
- Reversible: changes disappear when the agent is removed → normal function & morphology.
- Irreversible: agent persists or is sudden & severe → progresses to cell death.

</div>

**أسئلة Short essay**

<details><summary>Q1. Differentiate between reversible and irreversible cell injury.</summary>

**Answer:** In reversible injury the cellular changes disappear when the injurious agent is removed and the cell returns to normal function and morphology. In irreversible injury the agent persists, or is sudden and severe, so the changes do not return to normal and progress to cell death.

**ليش؟** هذا التعريف الحرفي من الملزمة، ركّز على كلمة persist و sudden and severe.
</details>

<details><summary>Q2. What two features of the injurious agent push a cell toward irreversible injury?</summary>

**Answer:** Persistence of the injurious agent, or an injury that is sudden and severe.

**ليش؟** الاستمرار أو الشدة المفاجئة يمنعون الخلية من الرجوع.
</details>

<details><summary>Q3. What happens to a reversibly injured cell once the injurious agent is removed?</summary>

**Answer:** The changes disappear and the cell returns to its normal function and morphology (stable baseline state).

**ليش؟** هذا هو تعريف الـ reversible بالضبط.
</details>

## 3. Causes of cell injury

الملزمة ذكرت 8 أسباب، احفظهن كقائمة:

1. **Oxygen deprivation (hypoxia, ischemia)** — ⭐ **most common cause**
2. **Chemical agents** — poisons, drugs, **carbon monoxide**, air pollution
3. **Infectious agents** — viruses, bacteria…
4. **Immunological reactions** — مثل **anaphylaxis** و **autoimmune diseases**
5. **Genetic defects** — مثل **Down syndrome**، **sickle cell anaemia**
6. **Nutritional imbalance** — **protein calorie malnutrition**، specific vitamin deficiency، وحتى **obesity** (يعني الزيادة هم سبب مو بس النقص!)
7. **Physical agents** — trauma، radiation، electric shock
8. **Aging**

> ⚠️ **انتبه:** السؤال الكلاسيكي "What is the most common cause of cell injury?" → **Oxygen deprivation (hypoxia and ischemia)**. وخلي ببالك إن **obesity** محسوبة ضمن nutritional imbalance.

<div dir="ltr" class="key-points">

**Key points**
- Most common cause: oxygen deprivation (hypoxia, ischemia).
- Others: chemical, infectious, immunological, genetic, nutritional (including obesity), physical agents, aging.

</div>

**Answer:** Oxygen deprivation (hypoxia, ischemia) — the most common; chemical agents; infectious agents; immunological reactions; genetic defects; nutritional imbalance; physical agents; aging.

**ليش؟** سؤال تعداد مضمون، وأول نقطة لازم تذكر إنها الأشيع.
</details>

**Answer:** Immunological: anaphylaxis and autoimmune diseases. Genetic: Down syndrome and sickle cell anaemia.

**ليش؟** الأمثلة نفسها المذكورة بالملزمة.
</details>

**Answer:** Nutritional imbalance: protein-calorie malnutrition, specific vitamin deficiency, obesity. Physical agents: trauma, radiation, electric shock.

**ليش؟** الملزمة تحسب الـ obesity كسبب، وهذا نقطة ممكن يلعب عليها السؤال.
</details>

## 4. Mechanisms of cell injury (الخمسة المهمين)

هذا أهم جزء بالملزمة. اكو 5 ميكانيزمات:

### 4.1 Depletion of ATP
لما ينزل الـ ATP لـ **أقل من 5–10% من الطبيعي** يصير تأثير واسع على أنظمة الخلية:

- الـ **sodium pump** (ouabain-sensitive **Na⁺, K⁺-ATPase**) يقل نشاطه → الصوديوم يدخل للخلية وياه ماي.
- **Cellular energy metabolism** يتغير.
- فشل الـ **Ca²⁺ pump** → **influx of Ca²⁺** يخرب هواية مكونات بالخلية.
- **Reduction in protein synthesis**.
- بالخلايا المحرومة من الأوكسجين أو الگلوكوز، البروتينات تصير **misfolded**، وهاي تحفز رد فعل اسمه **unfolded protein response** اللي ممكن يؤدي لـ injury وحتى death.

### 4.2 Mitochondrial damage
الـ mitochondria هي **الهدف لأغلب أنواع الـ injury**، وسلامتها critical لحياة الخلية. تتضرر بسبب:

1. **Increase intracellular Ca²⁺**
2. **Accumulation of intracellular free oxygen radicals**
3. **Breakdown of phospholipids by phospholipase**

### 4.3 Influx of intracellular calcium (loss of Ca homeostasis)
زيادة الـ Ca²⁺ داخل الخلية تشغّل إنزيمات تخريبية:

| Enzyme activated | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Phospholipases** | membrane damage |
| **Proteases** | degrade structural and membrane proteins |
| **ATPase** | يستهلك الـ ATP أكثر |
| **Endonucleases** | **fragmentation of genetic material** |

### 4.4 Accumulation of oxygen-derived free radicals (Oxidative stress)
- الـ free radical عنده **single unpaired electron in an outer orbital** → كلش **unstable** ويتفاعل بسهولة.
- **Reactive oxygen species (ROS)** هي byproducts طبيعية للـ **mitochondrial respiration**، مثل **superoxide (O₂•⁻)**، **hydrogen peroxide (H₂O₂)**، **hydroxyl radical (OH•)**. بالحالة الطبيعية الـ defense mechanisms تشيلها.
- إذا زاد إنتاجها أكثر من قابلية التخلص منها → **oxidative stress**.
- أمثلة حالات: **ischemia-reperfusion injury**، chemical injury، radiation injury، O₂ toxicity، cellular aging، tissue injury by inflammatory cells.
- **3 reactions** تسبب الأذى:
  1. **Membrane lipid peroxidation**
  2. **DNA fragmentation**
  3. **Cross-linking of proteins**

### 4.5 Defects in membrane permeability
- **Direct damage:** bacterial toxin، viral protein، complement components.
- **Indirect (secondary):** ↓ **ATP synthesis**، و **calcium-mediated phospholipase activity**.

> ⚠️ **انتبه:** الـ Calcium يدخل بأكثر من ميكانيزم — يخرب الـ mitochondria، ويشغّل الإنزيمات، ويسبب membrane damage بالـ phospholipase. إذا انسأل "role of calcium in cell injury" اكتب جدول الإنزيمات الأربعة.

<div dir="ltr" class="key-points">

**Key points**
- 5 mechanisms: ATP depletion, mitochondrial damage, Ca²⁺ influx, free radicals (oxidative stress), membrane permeability defects.
- ATP < 5–10% → ↓Na⁺/K⁺-ATPase, Ca²⁺ influx, ↓protein synthesis, unfolded protein response.
- Mitochondria damaged by ↑Ca²⁺, free radicals, phospholipase.
- Ca²⁺ activates phospholipases, proteases, ATPase, endonucleases.
- Free radicals → lipid peroxidation, DNA fragmentation, protein cross-linking.

</div>

**أسئلة Short essay**

<details><summary>Q1. Explain the consequences of ATP depletion in cell injury.</summary>

**Answer:** When ATP falls below 5–10% of normal: reduced activity of the Na⁺/K⁺-ATPase pump; altered cellular energy metabolism; failure of the Ca²⁺ pump with influx of Ca²⁺ damaging cellular components; reduced protein synthesis; and in cells deprived of oxygen or glucose, misfolded proteins trigger the unfolded protein response, which may lead to injury and death.

**ليش؟** هاي خمس نقاط مباشرة من الملزمة ومعاها الرقم 5–10% اللي يحبونه.
</details>

<details><summary>Q2. What are the effects of increased intracellular calcium?</summary>

**Answer:** Activation of phospholipases (membrane damage), proteases (degrade structural and membrane proteins), ATPase (further ATP depletion) and endonucleases (fragmentation of genetic material). It also contributes to mitochondrial damage.

**ليش؟** الأربع إنزيمات هي جوهر السؤال.
</details>

<details><summary>Q3. Define oxidative stress and list the three reactions by which free radicals injure cells.</summary>

**Answer:** Oxidative stress is the condition in which production of reactive oxygen species (byproducts of mitochondrial respiration such as O₂•⁻, H₂O₂ and OH•) exceeds the cell's defence mechanisms. Free radicals injure cells by membrane lipid peroxidation, DNA fragmentation and cross-linking of proteins.

**ليش؟** التعريف + التفاعلات الثلاثة سؤال short essay كلاسيكي.
</details>

## 5. Factors affecting severity + Morphology of reversible injury

### شنو يحدد شدة الـ injury؟
1. **Type, duration and severity** of the injurious agent.
2. **Type, state, adaptability and genetic make-up** of the cell. مثال مهم: الـ **neurons** كلش حساسة (**3–5 minutes** ischemia تكفي)، بينما **skeletal muscles** و **fibroblasts** يتحملون **ساعات**.

### شكل الـ reversible injury تحت المجهر (2 patterns)

**1) Cellular swelling** — ⭐ **first manifestation of almost all forms of injury**
- **Grossly** (إذا كل خلايا العضو متأثرة): **pallor**، **increased turgor**، **increased weight**.
- **Microscopically**: **small clear vacuoles** بالـ cytoplasm تمثل **distended segments of endoplasmic reticulum** → نسميها **hydropic change** أو **vacuolar degeneration**.
- مثال بالصور: **swollen hepatocytes in viral hepatitis**.

**2) Fatty change**
- تصير بالـ **hypoxic injury**، toxins، و metabolic injury.
- تبين كـ **lipid vacuoles in the cytoplasm**.
- أكثر شي بالـ **hepatocytes** و **myocardial cells**.

وبصور الكلية (reversible ischemic injury): **surface blebs**، **increased eosinophilia of cytoplasm**، و swelling لبعض الخلايا. أما الـ necrosis: **loss of nuclei**، fragmentation، و leakage of contents.

<div dir="ltr" class="key-points">

**Key points**
- Severity depends on the agent (type, duration, severity) and the cell (type, state, adaptability, genetics); neurons die in 3–5 min, skeletal muscle/fibroblasts need hours.
- Cellular swelling = first sign of almost all injury; hydropic change / vacuolar degeneration (distended ER).
- Fatty change: lipid vacuoles, mainly hepatocytes and myocardium.

</div>

**أسئلة Short essay**

<details><summary>Q1. What factors determine the severity of cell injury? Give an example.</summary>

**Answer:** 1) The type, duration and severity of the injurious agent. 2) The type, state, adaptability and genetic make-up of the affected cell — e.g. neurons are injured after 3–5 minutes of ischemia, whereas skeletal muscle and fibroblasts need hours.

**ليش؟** مثال الـ neurons هو اللي يميز الجواب الكامل.
</details>

<details><summary>Q2. Describe the gross and microscopic features of cellular swelling.</summary>

**Answer:** It is the first manifestation of almost all forms of injury. Grossly (when all cells of an organ are affected): pallor, increased turgor and increased weight. Microscopically: small clear cytoplasmic vacuoles representing distended segments of endoplasmic reticulum — hydropic change or vacuolar degeneration.

**ليش؟** السؤال يريد gross + microscopic + الاسمين البديلين.
</details>

<details><summary>Q3. What is fatty change and where is it mainly seen?</summary>

**Answer:** A pattern of reversible injury seen in hypoxic, toxic and metabolic injury, appearing as lipid vacuoles in the cytoplasm, mainly in hepatocytes and myocardial cells.

**ليش؟** اذكر الأسباب الثلاثة والعضوين.
</details>

## 6. Cellular adaptations (Atrophy, Hypertrophy, Hyperplasia, Metaplasia)

الخلايا تقاوم التغيرات حولها (physiological أو pathological) بتغييرات نسميها **adaptive responses**. أربعة:

### 6.1 Atrophy
- **Shrinkage in cell size** بسبب **loss of cell substance**. إذا عدد كافي من الخلايا تأثر، العضو كله يصغر.
- ⭐ الخلايا الـ atrophied **مو ميتة** (still viable).
- **Causes (6):** 1) decrease in workload (مثل immobilization of a limb)، 2) loss of innervation، 3) decrease in blood supply، 4) inadequate nutrition، 5) loss of endocrine stimulation، 6) aging.
- **Lipofuscin**: **finely granular yellow-brown pigment** بالـ cytoplasm (مثلاً myocardial fibers)، يمثل **indigestible residues of autophagic vacuoles** تتكون بالـ aging والـ atrophy.
- مثال: **brain atrophy** (aging + atherosclerosis) → **narrow gyri and wide sulci**.

### 6.2 Hypertrophy
- **Increase in the size of cells** → يكبر العضو.
- عادةً بالـ **non-dividing cells**.
- السبب: **hormonal stimulation** أو **increased functional demand**.
- **Physiologic**: skeletal muscle hypertrophy in weight lifters. **Pathologic**: **myocardial hypertrophy**.

### 6.3 Hyperplasia
- **Increase in the number of cells** (عادةً **dividing cells**) → يكبر العضو.
- الـ hypertrophy والـ hyperplasia هواية مرات يصيرن سوا.
- **Physiological:**
  1. **Hormonal** — glandular proliferation of the **female breast in pregnancy**.
  2. **Compensatory** — لما جزء من النسيج ينشال أو يمرض.
- **Pathological:**
  1. **Excessive hormonal stimulation** — **endometrial hyperplasia**.
  2. **Growth factor stimulation** — **skin wart** by **papilloma virus** (increased transcription factors).
- أسبابها: hormonal stimulation، increased functional demand، chronic stress or injury.
- مثال: hyperplasia of cervical epithelium → الخلايا عددها أكثر لكن **normal morphology**.

### 6.4 Metaplasia
- **Reversible** change: **differentiated cell type (epithelial or mesenchymal)** ينبدل بنوع ثاني.
- **Most common**: **columnar → squamous** بالـ **respiratory tract** بسبب **chronic irritation**.
- **Squamous → columnar**: **Barrett esophagus** — الـ squamous epithelium ينبدل بـ **intestinal-like columnar cells** بسبب **refluxed gastric acid**.
- **Connective tissue metaplasia**: تكوّن cartilage، bone، أو adipose tissue بمكان ما بي — مثل عظم بالعضلة = **myositis ossificans**.
- **Mechanism**: **reprogramming of local tissue stem cells** أو **colonization by differentiated cells from adjacent sites**.
- ⚠️ إذا استمرت، ممكن **تبدي malignant transformation**.

> ⚠️ **انتبه (فخ مشهور):** Hypertrophy = **size** وبالـ **non-dividing** cells. Hyperplasia = **number** وبالـ **dividing** cells. والأشيع بالـ metaplasia هو **columnar → squamous** مو العكس. Barrett هو العكس (squamous → columnar).

<div dir="ltr" class="key-points">

**Key points**
- Atrophy: ↓cell size, cells still viable; 6 causes; lipofuscin = yellow-brown wear-and-tear pigment.
- Hypertrophy: ↑cell size, non-dividing cells; physiologic (weight lifters) vs pathologic (myocardium).
- Hyperplasia: ↑cell number, dividing cells; physiologic (hormonal: breast in pregnancy; compensatory) vs pathologic (endometrial hyperplasia; wart by papilloma virus).
- Metaplasia: reversible replacement of one differentiated cell type by another; columnar→squamous most common; Barrett = squamous→columnar; myositis ossificans; may become malignant.

</div>

**أسئلة Short essay**

<details><summary>Q1. Define atrophy and list its causes.</summary>

**Answer:** Atrophy is shrinkage in cell size caused by loss of cell substance; when enough cells are involved the whole organ diminishes in size (atrophic cells are not dead). Causes: decreased workload, loss of innervation, decreased blood supply, inadequate nutrition, loss of endocrine stimulation, and aging.

**ليش؟** لا تنسى جملة "atrophied cells are not dead yet" — تفرّق الجواب.
</details>

<details><summary>Q2. Compare hypertrophy and hyperplasia, with a physiological and pathological example of each.</summary>

**Answer:** Hypertrophy is an increase in cell size, usually in non-dividing cells (physiological: skeletal muscle in weight lifters; pathological: myocardial hypertrophy). Hyperplasia is an increase in cell number, usually in dividing cells (physiological: breast in pregnancy, compensatory hyperplasia; pathological: endometrial hyperplasia, skin wart by papilloma virus). Both enlarge the organ and often occur together.

**ليش؟** السؤال المقارن هذا من أكثر الأسئلة تكراراً.
</details>

<details><summary>Q3. Define metaplasia and give three examples. What is its significance?</summary>

**Answer:** Metaplasia is a reversible change in which one differentiated cell type (epithelial or mesenchymal) is replaced by another. Examples: columnar to squamous in the respiratory tract (chronic irritation) — the most common; squamous to columnar in Barrett oesophagus (gastric acid reflux); myositis ossificans (bone in muscle). It results from reprogramming of local stem cells or colonization from adjacent sites, and if persistent may initiate malignant transformation.

**ليش؟** الأهمية (malignant transformation) هي النقطة اللي تاخذ عليها الدرجة الأخيرة.
</details>

## 7. Irreversible cell injury & Necrosis

### Point of no return
مع استمرار الأذى الخلية توصل لنقطة ما ترجع منها. ماكو marker واحد أكيد، بس دائماً اكو **3 phenomena**:
1. **Inability to restore mitochondrial function**
2. **Loss of structure and function of cell membrane**
3. **Loss of DNA and chromatin structural integrity**

والـ irreversible injury نوعين: **Necrosis** و **Apoptosis**.

### Necrosis
- **Definition:** death of **groups of contiguous cells** in a tissue or organ.
- يسبب **tissue dysfunction** واضح، و ⭐ **always pathologic**.
- مميزاته: **denaturation of cellular proteins**، **leakage of cellular contents** through damaged membranes، **local inflammation**، و **enzymatic digestion**.
- تسرّب البروتينات للدم هو أساس الـ blood tests. أحسن مثال: **cardiac-specific troponins** تبين بالدم من **ساعتين** بعد myocardial necrosis — قبل ما يبين الـ MI بالـ histology.

### Morphology of the necrotic cell

**1) Cytoplasmic changes**
- **Increased eosinophilia** — بسبب **loss of RNA (basophilia)** وزيادة ارتباط الـ eosin بالبروتينات الـ denatured.
- **Glassy homogenous** appearance — بسبب **loss of glycogen particles**.
- بعدين الـ cytoplasm يصير **vacuolated** و **"moth-eaten"** لأن الـ lysosomal enzymes تهضم الـ organelles.
- أخيراً ممكن **calcification**، والخلايا الميتة ممكن تتبدل بـ **myelin figures** (large whorled phospholipid masses).

**2) Nuclear changes** (3 patterns، non-specific breakdown of DNA)
- **Pyknosis**: nuclear **shrinkage** و **increased basophilia** (DNA condenses).
- **Karyorrhexis**: الـ pyknotic nucleus **يتفتت (fragmentation)**.
- **Karyolysis**: الـ basophilia **تبهت (fades)** بسبب **DNase activity**.

> ⚠️ **انتبه:** احفظ الترتيب: **Pyknosis = shrink**، **Karyorrhexis = fragment**، **Karyolysis = fade**. وسبب الـ eosinophilia مزدوج: loss of RNA + binding to denatured proteins.

<div dir="ltr" class="key-points">

**Key points**
- Irreversibility: can't restore mitochondria, membrane damage, loss of DNA/chromatin integrity.
- Necrosis: death of contiguous cells, always pathologic, with inflammation; leakage basis of blood tests (troponin within 2 h).
- Cytoplasm: ↑eosinophilia, glassy, moth-eaten, calcification, myelin figures.
- Nucleus: pyknosis (shrink), karyorrhexis (fragment), karyolysis (fade, DNase).

</div>

**أسئلة Short essay**

<details><summary>Q1. What three phenomena characterize irreversible cell injury?</summary>

**Answer:** Inability to restore mitochondrial function; loss of structure and function of the cell membrane; and loss of DNA and chromatin structural integrity.

**ليش؟** هاي الثلاثة اللي الملزمة قالت "consistently characterized".
</details>

<details><summary>Q2. Define necrosis and explain why troponin is useful clinically.</summary>

**Answer:** Necrosis is the death of groups of contiguous cells in a tissue or organ; it is always pathologic and features protein denaturation, leakage of cellular contents, local inflammation and enzymatic digestion. Leakage of intracellular proteins into the blood is the basis of tissue-specific blood tests: cardiac troponins appear as early as 2 hours after myocardial necrosis, before histological evidence of infarction.

**ليش؟** ربط التعريف بالـ troponin يبين إنك فاهم.
</details>

<details><summary>Q3. Describe the nuclear changes in necrosis.</summary>

**Answer:** Pyknosis — nuclear shrinkage and increased basophilia (DNA condenses into a solid mass); karyorrhexis — fragmentation of the pyknotic nucleus; karyolysis — fading of chromatin basophilia due to DNase activity.

**ليش؟** الثلاث أسماء مع وصف كل واحد = درجة كاملة.
</details>

## 8. Types (patterns) of necrosis

| Type | Key feature | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Coagulative** | protein denaturation primary; **structural outlines preserved**، nucleus lost؛ sudden severe ischemia | ⭐ **myocardial infarction**، kidney infarct (**wedge-shaped**) |
| **Gangrenous** | مو نوع مستقل — مصطلح جراحي: coagulative necrosis of a **limb** ± superimposed bacterial infection | **dry gangrene** (frostbite، foot)؛ **wet gangrene** (strangulated intestine، volvulus) |
| **Liquefactive** | **complete enzymatic digestion** → cyst مليان debris و fluid | **brain infarct**، **abscess** |
| **Fat** | action of **lipase** بالـ adipose؛ fatty acids + calcium = **calcium soaps (saponification)** → **chalky white** | **acute pancreatitis** (mesentery) |
| **Caseous** | **cheesy white**؛ microscopically **structureless amorphous pink debris** محاط بـ **granulomatous inflammation**؛ **architecture completely obliterated** | **tuberculosis** (lung، lymph node) |
| **Fibrinoid** | immune complexes + leaked plasma proteins بجدار الأوعية → **bright pink amorphous** بالـ H&E | immune reactions of vessels، مثل **polyarteritis nodosa** |

> ⚠️ **انتبه:** الفرق الذهبي: **Coagulative = architecture preserved**، **Caseous = architecture completely obliterated**. والـ brain دائماً **liquefactive** مو coagulative.

<div dir="ltr" class="key-points">

**Key points**
- Coagulative: preserved outlines, MI/kidney infarct.
- Gangrenous: surgical term, coagulative necrosis of a limb ± infection (dry vs wet).
- Liquefactive: brain infarcts and abscesses.
- Fat: lipase, saponification, chalky white — acute pancreatitis.
- Caseous: cheesy, TB, granuloma, architecture lost.
- Fibrinoid: immune complexes in vessel walls, bright pink.

</div>

**أسئلة Short essay**

<details><summary>Q1. Compare coagulative and caseous necrosis.</summary>

**Answer:** Coagulative necrosis: protein denaturation is the primary event, structural outlines of the cells are preserved while the nucleus is lost; caused by sudden severe ischemia (e.g. myocardial infarction, kidney). Caseous necrosis: cheesy white gross appearance, seen in the centre of tuberculous infection; microscopically structureless amorphous pink debris surrounded by granulomatous inflammation, and the tissue architecture is completely obliterated.

**ليش؟** الـ architecture هو مفتاح المقارنة.
</details>

<details><summary>Q2. Describe fat necrosis and its gross appearance.</summary>

**Answer:** A specific pattern in adipose tissue caused by the action of lipase, most commonly in acute pancreatitis. Liberated fatty acids combine with calcium (saponification) to form calcium soaps, seen grossly as chalky white areas.

**ليش؟** lipase + saponification + chalky white = الجواب الكامل.
</details>

<details><summary>Q3. Where is liquefactive necrosis seen and what is its outcome? What is gangrenous necrosis?</summary>

**Answer:** Liquefactive necrosis occurs in brain infarcts and abscesses; dead cells are completely digested by enzymes and the area becomes a cyst filled with debris and fluid. Gangrenous necrosis is not a distinct form of cell death but a surgical term for coagulative necrosis of a limb, with superimposed bacterial infection in wet gangrene.

**ليش؟** نقطة إن gangrenous "not a distinctive form" تجي كثير.
</details>

## 9. Apoptosis

- **Definition:** **programmed cell death** — يشيل الخلايا غير المرغوبة بتفعيل سلسلة أحداث **internally programmed** ومنسقة بـ gene products (**internal suicidal program**). يعني **cellular suicide**.
- هو **"the physiological way for a cell to die"**.

**أمثلة (physiological و pathological):**
1. **Embryogenesis** — تشكيل الأعضاء.
2. **Hormone-dependent involution** — مثل endometrium بالـ menstrual cycle.
3. **Cell deletion in proliferating populations** — مثل **intestinal crypt epithelium**.
4. **Deletion of autoreactive T cells in the thymus**.
5. **Mild injurious stimuli** (heat، radiation، drugs) تسبب **irreparable DNA damage** → تحفز apoptosis.

**Morphology:**
- يصيب **single cells or small clusters**.
- الخلية **round/oval** مع **intense eosinophilic cytoplasm**.
- **Condensed nuclear chromatin** يتجمع تحت الـ nuclear membrane، وبعدين **karyorrhexis**.
- الخلية **تنكمش**، تسوي **cytoplasmic buds**، وتتفتت لـ **apoptotic bodies** → تنبلع بسرعة (phagocytosed).

### Necrosis vs Apoptosis (من كلام الملزمة)

| | Necrosis | Apoptosis |
|---|---|---|
| Cells involved | groups of contiguous cells | single cells / small clusters |
| Nature | always pathologic | physiological (and some pathological) |
| Inflammation | local inflammation | لا يوجد، الأجزاء تنبلع بسرعة |
| Mechanism | protein denaturation، leakage، enzymatic digestion | internal suicidal program |
| Cell size | swelling، leakage | shrinkage → apoptotic bodies |

> ⚠️ **انتبه:** "Necrosis is **always** pathologic" بس الـ apoptosis ممكن physiological أو pathological. والـ karyorrhexis يصير بالاثنين!

<div dir="ltr" class="key-points">

**Key points**
- Apoptosis = programmed cell death (internal suicidal program), the physiological way to die.
- Examples: embryogenesis, endometrial involution, intestinal crypts, autoreactive T cells in thymus, irreparable DNA damage.
- Morphology: single cells, eosinophilic, condensed peripheral chromatin, karyorrhexis, shrinkage, apoptotic bodies, phagocytosis, no inflammation.

</div>

**أسئلة Short essay**

<details><summary>Q1. Define apoptosis and list its physiological and pathological examples.</summary>

**Answer:** Apoptosis is programmed cell death eliminating unwanted cells by activating a coordinated, internally programmed series of events (internal suicidal program). Examples: embryogenesis; hormone-dependent involution (endometrium in the menstrual cycle); cell deletion in proliferating populations (intestinal crypts); deletion of autoreactive T cells in the thymus; mild injurious stimuli causing irreparable DNA damage.

**ليش؟** التعريف + الأمثلة الخمسة كاملة.
</details>

<details><summary>Q2. Describe the morphology of apoptosis.</summary>

**Answer:** Affects single cells or clusters, which appear as round/oval masses with intensely eosinophilic cytoplasm and condensed chromatin aggregating at the periphery under the nuclear membrane, followed by karyorrhexis. The cell shrinks, forms cytoplasmic buds and fragments into apoptotic bodies that are rapidly phagocytosed.

**ليش؟** التسلسل (condensation → karyorrhexis → shrinkage → apoptotic bodies → phagocytosis) هو المطلوب.
</details>

<details><summary>Q3. Differentiate between necrosis and apoptosis.</summary>

**Answer:** Necrosis involves groups of contiguous cells, is always pathologic, with protein denaturation, leakage of contents, enzymatic digestion and local inflammation. Apoptosis involves single cells, is often physiological (internal suicidal program), the cell shrinks and forms apoptotic bodies that are phagocytosed without inflammation.

**ليش؟** سؤال مقارنة مضمون تقريباً بكل امتحان.
</details>

## 🔥 الخلاصة النهائية — احفظها قبل الامتحان

1. **Pathology** = logos + pathos؛ 4 aspects: **etiology, pathogenesis, morphologic changes, clinical significance**.
2. Stress → **adaptation** → if exceeded → **reversible injury** → persistent/severe → **irreversible** → **cell death**.
3. **Most common cause** of cell injury: **oxygen deprivation (hypoxia, ischemia)**.
4. **5 mechanisms**: ATP depletion (<5–10%)، mitochondrial damage، **Ca²⁺ influx**، **free radicals**، membrane permeability defects.
5. **Ca²⁺ activates**: phospholipases، proteases، ATPase، endonucleases.
6. **Free radicals** → lipid peroxidation، DNA fragmentation، protein cross-linking.
7. **Neurons**: 3–5 min ischemia؛ skeletal muscle/fibroblasts: hours.
8. **Cellular swelling** = first sign؛ **hydropic change/vacuolar degeneration**؛ **fatty change** in liver & heart.
9. **Atrophy** (↓size، cells alive، **lipofuscin**)؛ **Hypertrophy** (↑size، non-dividing)؛ **Hyperplasia** (↑number، dividing)؛ **Metaplasia** (reversible cell-type replacement، columnar→squamous most common، **Barrett**، **myositis ossificans**، may turn malignant).
10. Irreversibility: **mitochondria + membrane + DNA/chromatin**.
11. **Necrosis**: groups of cells، **always pathologic**، inflammation، **troponin after 2 h**.
12. Nuclear changes: **Pyknosis (shrink) → Karyorrhexis (fragment) → Karyolysis (fade)**.
13. Necrosis types: **Coagulative** (outlines preserved، MI)، **Liquefactive** (brain، abscess)، **Gangrenous** (limb، surgical term)، **Fat** (pancreatitis، saponification، chalky white)، **Caseous** (TB، cheesy، architecture lost، granuloma)، **Fibrinoid** (immune complexes in vessels، bright pink).
14. **Apoptosis**: programmed، single cells، **apoptotic bodies**، no inflammation؛ examples: embryogenesis، endometrium، intestinal crypts، **autoreactive T cells in thymus**، DNA damage.
