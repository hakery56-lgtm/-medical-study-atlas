## 1. Skin overview and epidermis

- **Skin = epidermis (outer) + dermis, connected to underlying tissue by the subcutaneous layer (hypodermis).**
- **Integumentary system:** skin, hair, nails, **sebaceous, eccrine, apocrine, mammary and ceruminous glands**; **functions: protection, temperature regulation, waste excretion, sensation, nutrition (vitamin D), wound healing.**
- **Epidermis (5 strata, deep → superficial):** **1 basale (germinativum), 2 spinosum, 3 granulosum, 4 lucidum (thick skin only), 5 corneum.**
- **Thin skin:** 4 layers, thin corneum. **Thick skin:** 5 layers with distinct **lucidum**, thick corneum (**palms and soles** — standard teaching).
- **Four epidermal cell types:** **keratinocytes** (most numerous), **melanocytes** (melanin, UV protection), **Langerhans "dendritic" cells** (antigen presenting), **Merkel cells** (touch, with sensory nerve endings) — functions are standard teaching.

<div class="key-points">

**Key points**
- Basale → spinosum → granulosum → lucidum (thick only) → corneum.
- Four cell types: keratinocytes, melanocytes, Langerhans, Merkel.

</div>

**Short-essay questions**

## 2. Dermis

- **Dermis = connective tissue** (**cells, fibers, ground substance; blood and lymphatic vessels, nerves, glands**) that **supports the epidermis and binds it to the hypodermis**.
- **Thicker than the epidermis.**
- **Two layers: papillary** (just under the epidermis, forms dermal papillae) and **reticular** (deeper).

<div class="key-points">

**Key points**
- Dermis: papillary + reticular.
- Thicker than the epidermis.

</div>

**Short-essay questions**

<details><summary>Q1. Define the dermis.</summary>

**Answer:** A connective tissue with cells, fibers, ground substance, vessels, nerves and glands that supports the epidermis and binds it to the hypodermis.

**Why:** Slide 12.
</details>

<details><summary>Q2. Name its layers.</summary>

**Answer:** The papillary layer and the reticular layer.

**Why:** Slide 12.
</details>

<details><summary>Q3. Compare dermis and epidermis thickness.</summary>

**Answer:** The dermis is thicker than the epidermis.

**Why:** Slide 12.

## 3. Hair and sebaceous gland

- **Hair follicle components:** **medulla, cortex, cuticle layer, internal root sheath, external root sheath, glassy membrane.** Also visible: **dermal papilla**, **hair bulb**, **arrector pili** muscle.
- **Sebaceous gland:** **simple branched acinar HOLOCRINE gland**, opens into the **lumen of the hair follicle**; pale foamy lipid-filled cells beside the arrector pili.

<div class="key-points">

**Key points**
- Six follicle components.
- Sebaceous = simple branched acinar holocrine.

</div>

**Short-essay questions**

<details><summary>Q1. List the components of a hair follicle.</summary>

**Answer:** Medulla, cortex, cuticle layer, internal root sheath, external root sheath and glassy membrane.

**Why:** Slide 13.
</details>

<details><summary>Q2. Describe the sebaceous gland.</summary>

**Why:** Slide 16.
</details>

<details><summary>Q3. What structures are seen next to the sebaceous gland?</summary>

**Why:** Slide 16.

## 4. Sweat glands

| Feature | Eccrine (merocrine) | Apocrine |
|---|---|---|
| **Site** | **Widespread** | **Axillary and genital regions** |
| **Lumen** | **Smaller** | **Much larger** |
| **Secretory part** | **Pale-staining; stratified cuboidal epithelium: clear cells, dark cells, myoepithelial cells** | **Simple cuboidal eosinophilic cells** |

<div class="key-points">

**Key points**
- Eccrine: clear, dark and myoepithelial cells.
- Apocrine: axilla and genital area, large lumen.

</div>

**Short-essay questions**

<details><summary>Q1. Describe the eccrine sweat gland.</summary>

**Answer:** Merocrine gland whose pale secretory part has stratified cuboidal epithelium with clear cells, dark cells and myoepithelial cells.

**Why:** Slide 18.
</details>

<details><summary>Q2. Describe the apocrine sweat gland.</summary>

**Answer:** Confined to the axillary and genital regions with a much larger lumen and a secretory part of simple cuboidal eosinophilic cells.

**Why:** Slide 20.
</details>

<details><summary>Q3. Compare the two types.</summary>

**Answer:** Eccrine is widespread with a smaller lumen and stratified secretory epithelium; apocrine is axillary/genital with a larger lumen and simple cuboidal secretory cells.

**Why:** Slides 18 and 20.
</details>

## ⚠️ Exam traps
- **Stratum lucidum = thick skin only.**
- **Sebaceous = HOLOCRINE; eccrine = MERocrine.**
- **Dermis papillary layer is superficial; reticular is deep.**
- The slide text calls the epidermis "four types of cells" but lists layers on another slide; keep the 5 strata and 4 cell types separate.
