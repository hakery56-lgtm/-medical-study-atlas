## 1. The core idea: 10 electrodes → 12 leads

- ⭐ **Placement is not a detail:** a small landmarking error can change the tracing you think you are interpreting. **Chest leads depend on precise anatomical landmarks**; **repeatability** matters when ECGs are compared over time. **Think: anatomy → electrode → lead → tracing.**
- **10 electrodes:** **RA, LA, RL, LL + V1–V6.** **RL = reference/ground** (not a displayed lead).
- **12 standard leads:** **frontal plane: I, II, III, aVR, aVL, aVF** (from the **4 limb electrodes**); **horizontal plane: V1–V6** (from the **6 chest electrodes**). A 12-lead ECG is **a set of electrical viewpoints, not 12 physical electrodes.**

### Einthoven's triangle
| Lead | Electrodes |
|---|---|
| **I** | **RA → LA** |
| **II** | **RA → LL** |
| **III** | **LA → LL** |
| **aVR / aVL / aVF** | Same limb electrode set, **different reference calculations** |

### Learning outcomes
- **Explain** why 10 electrodes give 12 leads; **locate V1–V6 using landmarks, not the nipple**; **apply limb electrodes consistently**; **reduce artifact**; **standardize positioning and documentation** for serial ECGs.

<div class="key-points">

**Key points**
- 10 electrodes (RA, LA, RL, LL, V1–V6) → 12 leads; RL = ground.
- Frontal: I, II, III, aVR, aVL, aVF; horizontal: V1–V6.
- I = RA→LA, II = RA→LL, III = LA→LL.

</div>

## 2. Before the patient: preparation and set-up

- **Explain the procedure and protect privacy/dignity;** **remove nearby electronic devices**; position the patient **supine or semi-Fowler** if tolerated; **relaxed shoulders, arms by the side, legs uncrossed**; **still and quiet** during acquisition; for larger patients an **arm across the abdomen** can reduce muscle tension.
- **Movement → muscle artifact; phone/equipment → electrical interference.** Goal: **a relaxed patient + reproducible position.**

### Skin preparation (low impedance, no irritation)
- **DRY:** remove sweat and moisture. **HAIR:** shave hair that prevents full contact. **OIL:** clean away oils/lotions. **CONTACT:** gentle prep/abrasion when appropriate.

### Electrode application
1. **Gel:** fresh, adequately moist conductive gel.
2. **Storage:** keep electrodes sealed; prevent gel dry-out.
3. **Surface:** avoid **bones, incisions, irritated skin, high-motion areas.**
4. **Consistency:** prefer **the same brand/type** for a recording.
5. **Connection:** secure the patient cable and check each connector. **Full contact = better signal.**

<div class="key-points">

**Key points**
- Supine/semi-Fowler, relaxed, still, privacy, devices away.
- Skin: dry, shave hair, remove oil, gentle abrasion; gel fresh, avoid bones/incisions.
- Movement = muscle artifact; electrical devices = interference.

</div>

## 3. Chest electrodes V1–V6 (landmarks)

- ⭐ **Landmark first, electrode second. Do not guess V1/V2.**
  1. Palpate the **sternal angle (Angle of Louis)**; 2. identify the **2nd rib**; 3. count down to the **4th intercostal space (ICS)**; 4. use it for **V1 and V2.**

| Electrode | Position |
|---|---|
| **V1** | **4th ICS, RIGHT sternal border** |
| **V2** | **4th ICS, LEFT sternal border** (memory hook: **V1 = right, V2 = left**) |
| **V4** | **5th ICS, midclavicular line** — **place V4 first: it is the level setter** |
| **V3** | **Midway between V2 and V4** (not an anatomical landmark) |
| **V5** | **Anterior axillary line**, **same horizontal level as V4** |
| **V6** | **Mid-axillary line**, **same horizontal level as V4** |

- **Chest lead rule: landmark → V4 → V3 → V5/V6.** Move **laterally** from V4; **do not follow the rib space** up or down.
- **Horizontal lock:** placing V4–V6 along the **curve of the ribs** instead of a straight horizontal line **artificially alters R-wave progression** and creates interpretive errors. **Faculty caution:** use anatomical landmarks; **do not rely on memorized visual distances alone.**

### Special anatomy
- Use **landmarks, not the nipple** (its position varies). For patients with breasts, place **V3–V6 beneath the breast tissue** when necessary to reach the true landmarks. **Landmarks > visual symmetry.**

<div class="key-points">

**Key points**
- Sternal angle → 2nd rib → 4th ICS: V1 right, V2 left sternal border.
- V4 = 5th ICS midclavicular (place first); V3 = midpoint V2–V4; V5/V6 = anterior/mid-axillary, level with V4.
- Never use the nipple; horizontal lock prevents R-wave progression errors.

</div>

**Short-essay questions**

<details><summary>Q1. How do you find and place V1 and V2?</summary>

**Answer:** Palpate the sternal angle, identify the 2nd rib, count down to the 4th intercostal space; V1 goes at the right sternal border and V2 at the left sternal border in that space.

**Why:** Slides "Find the 4th intercostal space first" and "V1 and V2".
</details>

<details><summary>Q2. Why is V4 placed first, and where are V3, V5 and V6?</summary>

**Answer:** V4 (5th ICS, midclavicular line) is the level setter: V5 (anterior axillary line) and V6 (mid-axillary line) must be on the same horizontal level, and V3 lies midway between V2 and V4.

**Why:** Slides "V4 first", "V3", "V5 + V6".
</details>

<details><summary>Q3. What is the horizontal lock and why does it matter?</summary>

**Answer:** V5 and V6 are locked to the horizontal level of V4; following the rib curve instead alters R-wave progression and causes interpretive errors.

**Why:** Slide "The V4–V6 horizontal lock".
</details>

## 4. Limb electrodes, artifact and color coding

### Limb electrodes
- **RA** and **LA: between shoulder and elbow**; **RL** and **LL: below the torso and above the ankle.** **Keep the placement strategy UNIFORM.** They create the **six frontal leads.** *Variation:* limb electrodes may be placed on the **proximal torso only when permitted by the device/protocol**; altered placement can change the tracing.

### Artifact troubleshooting
- Switch off **non-essential electrical equipment**; look for **cable loops and cables near metal**; inspect leads/cables for **cracks or poor connections**; confirm the patient cable is firmly connected; use appropriate **filters/preamplifier settings.**
- **Noisy trace: check environment → cables → electrodes → patient movement. Fix the source, then repeat.**

### Color coding
| System | RA / R | LA / L | RL / N | LL / F |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **AHA** | **White** | **Black** | **Green** | **Red** |
| **IEC** | **Red** | **Yellow** | **Black** | **Green** |

- AHA mnemonic: **"Smoke over fire • Snow over grass."** **Always confirm the device/system before relying on color memory.**

<div class="key-points">

**Key points**
- RA/LA between shoulder and elbow; RL/LL between torso and ankle; keep uniform.
- Noisy trace: environment → cables → electrodes → patient movement.
- AHA: RA white, LA black, RL green, LL red; IEC: R red, L yellow, N black, F green.

</div>

## 5. Best practices, common errors and the checklist

### Best practices
- **PRIVACY** (drape, minimize exposure); **CONSISTENCY** (same placement and position for serial ECGs); **DOCUMENT** symptoms such as chest pain on the tracing/notes; **STANDARDIZE** (same landmarks each time).

### Common errors and fixes
| Error | Effect | Correction |
|---|---|---|
| **V1/V2 placed too high** | **May mimic or obscure pathology** | **Palpate sternal angle → 4th ICS; reposition** |
| **V4–V6 not horizontal** | **Changes R-wave progression** | **Align V5/V6 to V4 level, ignoring rib curve** |
| **Loose electrodes / oily skin** | **Artifact, unstable baseline** | **Clean/dry skin; replace electrodes** |
| **Patient talking, shivering, moving** | **Muscle artifact** | **Reassure, keep warm, request silence** |
| **Reversed limb leads** | **Abnormal axis and polarity patterns** | **Verify RA/LA/RL/LL mapping** |

- Other "almost right" errors: **V4 placed by eye** (use 5th ICS + midclavicular line), **nipple used as landmark**, **asymmetric limb placement.** ⭐ **Rule of thumb: if quality is poor, do not print and pass it on; correct the cause and repeat when clinically appropriate.**

### 60-second pre-recording checklist
- Patient identified/explained/privacy → position comfortable and still → skin prepared with full contact → **V1/V2 at 4th ICS, V4 at 5th ICS midclavicular** → **V3 between V2 and V4; V5/V6 level with V4** → RA/LA/RL/LL consistent → cable secured, interference minimized → symptoms documented. **STOP → LANDMARK → APPLY → CHECK → RECORD.**

### Mini case: chest pain at triage
- First task is **a technically reliable ECG, not interpretation** ("technique first, then interpret"): **1) position + privacy, 2) landmark V1/V2, 3) place V4, 4) complete V3/V5/V6, 5) limb electrodes, 6) record + document symptoms.**

### Take-home algorithm
- **POSITION** (relaxed, still) → **LANDMARK** (4th ICS → V1/V2) → **ANCHOR** (5th ICS MCL → V4) → **CONNECT** (V3 between V2/V4) → **LEVEL** (V5/V6 with V4) → **LIMBS** (RA, LA, RL, LL) → **CHECK** (skin, cables, artifact) → **RECORD** (symptoms + reproducible setup).

### OSCE 10-point competency checklist
1. Introduces self, confirms identity, explains procedure, obtains consent. 2. Hand hygiene and PPE per risk. 3. Checks indication/order, allergies, contraindications and equipment. 4. Aseptic/non-touch technique and correct anatomical landmarks. 5. Performs the core procedure safely. 6. Uses safety devices and disposes of sharps immediately. 7. Checks patient response and recognizes complications. 8. Leaves patient comfortable and restores a safe environment. 9. Documents procedure and result accurately. 10. Escalates abnormal findings according to policy. **"Safe technique is a sequence — not a single action."**

### Standards
- **AHA/ACC** guidance on standardization/interpretation of the ECG; **IEC 60601-2-25** (safety and performance of electrocardiographs); Resuscitation Council and local ACS guidance; **manufacturer instructions.** **Always follow institutional SOPs** for ECG placement, infection control, privacy and escalation.

<div class="key-points">

**Key points**
- Mistakes (high V1/V2, non-horizontal V4–V6, loose electrodes, movement, reversed limbs) each have a specific fix.
- Checklist: STOP → LANDMARK → APPLY → CHECK → RECORD; mini case: technique first, then interpret.
- OSCE: 10-step sequence, ending in documentation and escalation.

</div>

## ⚠️ Exam traps
- **RL is the ground; it is not a displayed lead. 10 electrodes, 12 leads.**
- **V1 = RIGHT sternal border, V2 = LEFT (both 4th ICS). V4 = 5th ICS midclavicular.**
- **V3 = midpoint between V2 and V4; V5/V6 stay LEVEL with V4 (not following the ribs).**
- **Never use the nipple as a landmark.**
- **Place V4 BEFORE V3, V5, V6.**
- **AHA: RA white, LA black, RL green, LL red; IEC: R red, L yellow, N black, F green.**
- **Poor-quality tracing: fix the cause and repeat; do not just print it.**
- **Technique first, then interpretation.**
