## 3. MRI of the knee: menisci and cruciate ligaments

- **Sagittal MRI, medial (M) to lateral (L):** **body of the medial meniscus → horns of the medial meniscus → PCL → ACL → horns of the lateral meniscus → body of the lateral meniscus.**
- ⭐ **Medial meniscus:** **posterior horn wider and longer than the anterior horn.**
- ⭐ **Lateral meniscus:** **both horns symmetrical**; **lateral projection of the lateral condyle of the tibia** seen on the slides.
- **Collateral ligaments:** shown on **coronal MRI** next to a knee model.

<div class="key-points">

**Key points**
- Sagittal order M to L: medial meniscus, PCL, ACL, lateral meniscus.
- Medial meniscus: posterior horn larger. Lateral meniscus: symmetrical horns.
- Collateral ligaments are best seen on coronal images.

</div>

**Short-essay questions**

<details><summary>Q1. List the structures on sagittal knee MRI from medial to lateral.</summary>

**Answer:** Body and horns of the medial meniscus, PCL, ACL, horns and body of the lateral meniscus.

**Why:** Slide "MRI, sagittal section (M–L)".
</details>

<details><summary>Q2. How do you tell the medial and lateral menisci apart on sagittal MRI?</summary>

**Answer:** The medial meniscus has a posterior horn that is wider and longer than the anterior horn; the lateral meniscus has symmetrical horns.

**Why:** Slides "Medial meniscus" and "Lateral meniscus".
</details>

<details><summary>Q3. Which MRI plane is used to show the collateral ligaments?</summary>

**Answer:** The coronal plane.

**Why:** Slide "Collateral ligaments".
</details>

## ⚠️ Exam traps
- **Medial meniscus: posterior horn bigger; lateral meniscus: symmetrical horns.**
- **Fabella = lateral view; adductor tubercle = medial distal femur.**
- **Plateau (J, L) vs condyle (K, M) are separate labels on the tibia; P = fibular head, Q = fibular neck.**
- **Sagittal MRI order M → L: medial meniscus, PCL, ACL, lateral meniscus.**
- **PL, PB, PT are three different peroneal muscles; EDL is not a peroneal muscle.**
- **Many review slides have no key: do not guess beyond labeled content.**
