## 1. The eicosanoid pathway

- **Eicosanoids** include **prostaglandins, thromboxanes and leukotrienes**.
- ⭐ **Pathway:** arachidonic acid (AA) esterified in **membrane phospholipids** → released by **phospholipase A2** → **cyclooxygenases (COX)** → **prostanoids (prostaglandins, prostacyclin, thromboxane)**; **lipoxygenases (LOX)** → **leukotrienes and lipoxins**.
- **Two COX isozymes:** **COX-1** generates prostanoids for **"house-keeping"** (e.g. **gastric epithelial cytoprotection**); **COX-2** is the **major source of prostanoids in inflammation and cancer**.
- Lipoxygenase products: **LTB4, LTC4, LTD4, LTE4**; **LTC4 and LTD4 are potent bronchoconstrictors**.

<div class="key-points">

**Key points**
- AA → (COX) prostanoids; AA → (LOX) leukotrienes.
- COX-1 = house-keeping (gastric cytoprotection); COX-2 = inflammation and cancer.
- LTC4 and LTD4 = potent bronchoconstrictors.

</div>

## 2. Effects of prostaglandins and thromboxanes

| System | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Vascular smooth muscle** | **TXA2 and PGF2α** = potent **vasoconstrictors**; **PGI2 and PGE2** = **vasodilation** |
| **GIT** | **Longitudinal** muscle contracted by **PGE2, PGF2α**; **circular** muscle contracted strongly by **PGF2α** |
| **Airways** | **Relaxed** by **PGE2, PGI2**; **contracted** by **PGD2, PGF2α, TXA2** |
| **Platelets** | **PGE2**: low dose **enhances**, high dose **inhibits**; **PGD2, PGI2** inhibit; **TXA2** aggregates |
| **Kidney** | **PGE2, PGI2** ↑ **renin release and GFR** |
| **Female genital tract** | Uterus **contracted** by **PGF2α, TXA2**; high **PGE2** → **relaxation** |
| **Male** | Low seminal PG → relative **infertility**; **PGE1** enhances **erection** |
| **CNS / PNS** | **Fever, sleep, neurotransmission** |
| **Neuroendocrine** | **PGE2** → ↑ GH, prolactin, TSH, ACTH, FSH, LH; **LTC4, LTD4** → ↑ LH |
| **Bone** | ↑ **bone turnover** |
| **Eye** | **PGE and PGF derivatives** ↓ **intraocular pressure** |
| **Cancer** | **Angiogenesis** promoted by **COX-2-derived TXA2, PGE2, PGI2** |

<div class="key-points">

**Key points**
- Vasoconstrictors: TXA2, PGF2α; vasodilators: PGI2, PGE2; platelets: TXA2 aggregates, PGI2 and PGD2 inhibit.
- Airways: relaxed by PGE2/PGI2, contracted by PGD2/PGF2α/TXA2.
- Eye: PGE and PGF derivatives lower IOP; cancer: COX-2-derived eicosanoids promote angiogenesis.

</div>

**Short-essay questions**

<details><summary>Q1. Describe the vascular and platelet effects of prostaglandins and thromboxane.</summary>

**Answer:** TXA2 and PGF2α are potent vasoconstrictors, whereas PGI2 and PGE2 promote vasodilation. Low concentrations of PGE2 enhance platelet aggregation and higher ones inhibit it; PGD2 and PGI2 inhibit aggregation and TXA2 is a platelet aggregator.

**Why:** Slides "Effects of prostaglandins and thromboxanes" (smooth muscle, platelets).
</details>

<details><summary>Q2. Describe the effects on airways, kidney and reproductive organs.</summary>

**Answer:** Airway smooth muscle is relaxed by PGE2 and PGI2 and contracted by PGD2, PGF2α and TXA2; PGE2 and PGI2 increase renin release and GFR; the uterus is contracted by PGF2α and TXA2 (high PGE2 relaxes it); low seminal PG gives relative infertility and PGE1 enhances erection.

**Why:** Slides "Effects of prostaglandins and thromboxanes".
</details>

<details><summary>Q3. List the other effects of eicosanoids (CNS, neuroendocrine, bone, eye, cancer).</summary>

**Answer:** Fever, sleep and neurotransmission; PGE2 promotes release of GH, prolactin, TSH, ACTH, FSH and LH (LTC4/LTD4 stimulate LH); increased bone turnover; PGE and PGF derivatives lower IOP; COX-2-derived TXA2, PGE2 and PGI2 promote angiogenesis in cancer.

**Why:** Slide "Central and peripheral nervous systems … cancer".
</details>

## 3. Lipoxygenase products and inhibition of eicosanoid synthesis

- **Blood cells and inflammation:** **immunomodulators** and **chemotactic agents** for many inflammatory cells.
- **Heart and smooth muscle:** **LTC4, LTD4** ↓ **myocardial contractility and coronary blood flow**; **IBD** patients express substantially more **LTB4**; airways: **LTC4, LTD4 = potent bronchoconstrictors**.
- ⭐ **Inhibition of synthesis:** **corticosteroids block all known pathways**; **NSAIDs** (indomethacin, ibuprofen) **reversibly inhibit COX**.
- The lecture notes that **low-dose aspirin inhibits platelet aggregation while higher doses do not**, and asks the student to explain why (no answer on the slide).

<div class="key-points">

**Key points**
- Lipoxygenase products: chemotaxis/immunomodulation; LTC4 and LTD4 reduce cardiac function and constrict airways; LTB4 high in IBD.
- Corticosteroids block all eicosanoid pathways; NSAIDs reversibly inhibit COX.

</div>

**Short-essay questions**

<details><summary>Q1. List the effects of lipoxygenase products.</summary>

**Answer:** They act as immunomodulators and chemotactic agents for inflammatory cells; LTC4 and LTD4 reduce myocardial contractility and coronary blood flow and are potent bronchoconstrictors; LTB4 is substantially increased in inflammatory bowel disease.

**Why:** Slide "Effects of lipoxygenase products".
</details>

<details><summary>Q2. Compare how corticosteroids and NSAIDs inhibit eicosanoid synthesis.</summary>

**Answer:** Corticosteroids block all known pathways of eicosanoid synthesis; NSAIDs such as indomethacin and ibuprofen block prostaglandin formation by reversibly inhibiting COX.

**Why:** Slide "Inhibition of eicosanoid synthesis".
</details>

<details><summary>Q3. What does the lecture note about aspirin dose?</summary>

**Answer:** Low-dose aspirin inhibits platelet aggregation while higher doses do not; the slide poses the question "Why?" for discussion.

**Why:** Slide "Inhibition of eicosanoid synthesis" (note).
</details>

## 4. Clinical applications: reproductive and cardiovascular

| System | Drug | Use |
|---|---|---|
| **Female reproductive** | **Dinoprostone (PGE2)**, **carboprost (PGF2α)** | **Induce abortion**, control **postpartum hemorrhage** (vomiting, diarrhea, transient fever may occur) |
| | **Mifepristone** (antiprogestin) + **misoprostol (PGE1)** | **Early abortion** |
| | Prostaglandins | **Facilitate labor**; **dysmenorrhea** |
| **Male reproductive** | **Alprostadil (PGE1)** | **Impotence** |
| **Cardiovascular** | **Prostacyclin (PGI2)** | **Pulmonary hypertension** (↓ peripheral, pulmonary, coronary resistance); also porto-pulmonary hypertension |
| | **PGE and PGI2 compounds** | **Raynaud's phenomenon**, peripheral atherosclerosis |
| **Blood** | **Aspirin** | Inhibits **platelet COX-1**; prostacyclin is a powerful **vasodilator** and **inhibitor of platelet aggregation** |

<div class="key-points">

**Key points**
- Dinoprostone and carboprost: abortion and postpartum hemorrhage; mifepristone + misoprostol: early abortion.
- Alprostadil (PGE1): impotence; prostacyclin: pulmonary hypertension.
- PGE/PGI2: Raynaud's and peripheral atherosclerosis; aspirin inhibits platelet COX-1.

</div>

**Short-essay questions**

<details><summary>Q1. List the uses of prostaglandins in the female reproductive system.</summary>

**Answer:** Dinoprostone (PGE2) and carboprost (PGF2α) induce abortion and control postpartum hemorrhage (with vomiting, diarrhea and transient fever); mifepristone with misoprostol (PGE1) produces early abortion; prostaglandins also facilitate labor and treat dysmenorrhea.

**Why:** Slide "Clinical applications — female reproductive system".
</details>

<details><summary>Q2. What are alprostadil and prostacyclin used for?</summary>

**Answer:** Alprostadil (PGE1) is used for impotence; prostacyclin (PGI2) lowers peripheral, pulmonary and coronary resistance and is used for pulmonary hypertension and porto-pulmonary hypertension.

**Why:** Slides "Male reproductive system" and "Cardiovascular system".
</details>

<details><summary>Q3. What are the cardiovascular and blood roles of PGE, PGI2 and aspirin?</summary>

**Answer:** PGE and PGI2 compounds are used in Raynaud's phenomenon and peripheral atherosclerosis; prostacyclin is a powerful vasodilator and inhibitor of platelet aggregation; aspirin inhibits platelet COX-1.

**Why:** Slides "Peripheral vascular disease" and "Blood".
</details>

## 5. Clinical applications: respiratory, GIT, eye and immune system

| System | Drug | Use |
|---|---|---|
| **Respiratory** | **Zafirlukast, montelukast** (leukotriene-receptor inhibitors) | **Asthma control** |
| | **Zileuton** (lipoxygenase inhibitor) | Asthma |
| | **Corticosteroids** | Inhibit eicosanoid synthesis |
| | **Cromolyn** | Inhibits release of **eicosanoids, histamine and PAF** from **mast cells** |
| **GIT** | **Misoprostol (PGE1 derivative)** | **Cytoprotective**: prevents **peptic ulcer**; with mifepristone terminates early pregnancy |
| **Eye** | **Latanoprost, travoprost** (PGF2α derivatives) | **Open angle glaucoma** (topical) |
| **Immune system** | **PGI2** | Reversed **renal transplant rejection** in some cases |
| | **Corticosteroids** | **First-line** in **acute rejection** |
| | **NSAIDs** | **Inflammatory disorders** (arthritis, rheumatoid arthritis) |

<div class="key-points">

**Key points**
- Asthma: zafirlukast, montelukast (receptor blockers), zileuton (5-LOX inhibitor), cromolyn (mast cell stabilizer).
- Misoprostol: cytoprotective for peptic ulcer; latanoprost/travoprost: open angle glaucoma.
- Acute transplant rejection: corticosteroids first line.

</div>

**Short-essay questions**

<details><summary>Q1. List the antiasthma drugs affecting eicosanoids.</summary>

**Answer:** Zafirlukast and montelukast (leukotriene-receptor inhibitors), zileuton (lipoxygenase inhibitor), corticosteroids (inhibit eicosanoid synthesis) and cromolyn (inhibits release of eicosanoids, histamine and PAF from mast cells).

**Why:** Slide "Respiratory system".
</details>

<details><summary>Q2. What are misoprostol, latanoprost and travoprost used for?</summary>

**Answer:** Misoprostol (PGE1 derivative) is a cytoprotective agent preventing peptic ulcer (and with mifepristone terminates early pregnancy); latanoprost and travoprost are topical PGF2α derivatives used for open angle glaucoma.

**Why:** Slides "Gastrointestinal system" and "Glaucoma".
</details>

<details><summary>Q3. What are the immune-system applications?</summary>

**Answer:** PGI2 has reversed rejection in some renal transplant patients and corticosteroids are first-line for acute rejection; NSAIDs treat inflammatory disorders such as arthritis and rheumatoid arthritis.

**Why:** Slide "Immune system".
</details>

## ⚠️ Exam traps

- **COX-1 = house-keeping (gastric protection); COX-2 = inflammation and cancer.**
- **TXA2 = vasoconstrictor and platelet aggregator; PGI2 = vasodilator and platelet inhibitor.**
- **PGE2 relaxes airway smooth muscle; PGF2α, PGD2 and TXA2 contract it.**
- **Corticosteroids block ALL eicosanoid pathways; NSAIDs only COX (reversibly).**
- **Montelukast/zafirlukast block leukotriene receptors; zileuton inhibits lipoxygenase.**
- **Dinoprostone = PGE2; carboprost = PGF2α; misoprostol and alprostadil = PGE1.**
- **Latanoprost and travoprost are PGF2α derivatives for glaucoma.**
