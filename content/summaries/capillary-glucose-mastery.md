## 1. Definition, uses and limits

- ⭐ **Capillary random blood glucose** = **point-of-care measurement at any time**, interpreted with **symptoms, meals, treatment and clinical context.**
- **Can:** quickly show **possible low or high glucose** and support **immediate decisions.** **Cannot:** a **single meter result does not establish diabetes** by itself.
- **Low:** **< 70 mg/dL (3.9 mmol/L)** = common hypoglycaemia alert; **treat per protocol.** **High:** needs **context** (symptoms, known diabetes, meals, medicines, illness, **ketones**).
- **Meter vs laboratory:** meter needs **clinical correlation** and must **never delay emergency assessment**; **laboratory random plasma glucose ≥ 200 mg/dL (11.1 mmol/L) with classic symptoms or hyperglycaemic crisis** can establish diabetes (ADA 2026 as quoted); otherwise **confirmation** is needed.

## 2. Equipment and quality check

- **Equipment:** **gloves, approved meter, compatible in-date strips, alcohol/antiseptic wipes, gauze, single-use lancet, sharps container.**
- **Parts of the glucometer:** **strip port, display (test value, unit), buttons, lancet device (top and lancet), strip (blood-sampling end and machine end).**
- **Quality check:** **meter status, strip storage, expiry date, coding/lot, QC status, lancets (single-use, never shared, discard immediately).**

**Key points**
- Lancets are single-use, never shared.

## 3. The procedure

- **Core sequence:** **clinical question → prepare → measure → interpret → act and document.**

| Step | Action |
|---|---|
| **1** | **Hand hygiene** |
| **2** | **Identify the patient** |
| **3** | **Warm the hand** |
| **4** | **Prepare the meter** |
| **5** | **Clean and dry** (let antiseptic dry fully; if sugar may be on the hands, **wash with soap and water** and dry) |
| **6** | **Lance the side of the fingertip**, avoid excessive squeezing |
| **7** | **Apply the sample** per the meter's volume instructions |
| **8** | **Read and record**; **dispose of the lancet immediately**; clean the meter per the manufacturer |

<div class="key-points">

**Key points**
- Eight steps; lance the side of the fingertip.
- Dry the antiseptic fully before lancing.

</div>

**Short-essay questions**

<details><summary>Q1. List the steps of the procedure.</summary>

**Answer:** Hand hygiene, identify the patient, warm the hand, prepare the meter, clean and dry, lance the side of the fingertip, apply the sample, read and record.

**Why:** Slides 13-15.
</details>

<details><summary>Q2. How should the finger be prepared?</summary>

**Answer:** Clean with antiseptic and let it dry fully (wash with soap and water if sugar may be present), then lance the side of the fingertip without squeezing too hard.

**Why:** Slide 14.
</details>

<details><summary>Q3. What happens after the reading?</summary>

**Answer:** Record the result, dispose of the lancet immediately and clean the meter as instructed.

**Why:** Slide 15.

## 4. Errors, interpretation and cases

- **Why a reading can be wrong:** **patient** (squeezing too hard, cold or poorly perfused finger); **equipment** (expired or badly stored strips, shared or reused lancets); **technique** (wet alcohol, not repeating an odd result, missing time, units or symptoms).
- **Unexpected result:** **1 assess the patient → 2 repeat with correct technique (check device and strips) → 3 escalate per local protocol.**
- **Symptoms:** **low:** **sweaty, confused**; **high:** **polyuria, polydipsia, weight loss.** Link every number to **symptoms, meals, medicines, illness, ketones.**

| Case | Findings | Priority | Missing | Inform |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **A** | **Sweaty, confused; 55 mg/dL** | **Treat possible hypoglycaemia per protocol, stay with the patient; repeat after treatment** | Diabetes history, medicines, last meal | **Senior or doctor in charge** |
| **B** | **Polyuria, polydipsia, weight loss; 250 mg/dL** | **Assess the patient; check for ketones; repeat if in doubt without delaying care** | Known diabetes, medicines, illness, meals | **Doctor in charge** |

- **Key message:** **safe technique is a sequence:** **assess, prepare, perform, reassess, document.**

<div class="key-points">

**Key points**
- Assess first, then repeat, then escalate.
- Case A: treat hypoglycaemia; Case B: check ketones.

</div>
