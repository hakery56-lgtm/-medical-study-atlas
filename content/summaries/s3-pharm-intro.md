## 1. What is pharmacology? Drug vs medicine

- **Pharmacology** = the science that studies the **interactions between drugs and living systems**.
- It has two big branches:
  - **Pharmacodynamics** = effects of the **drug on the body**.
  - **Pharmacokinetics** = effects of the **body on the drug**.

### Drug vs medicine (as defined on the slides)
| Term | Definition on the slide |
|---|---|
| **Drug** | The **final dosage form** (tablet, capsule, ampule, syrup…) that can be given to the body; contains the **active ingredient** + **excipients** (inert ingredients such as sugar, starch). |
| **Medicine** | **Any substance that changes biologic function** when given to a living organism through its **chemical actions**; the change = **biological effect**. Usually a crude chemical compound. |

## 2. Pharmacodynamics — the types of drug effect

- **Therapeutic effect** = the effect of the drug on the **therapeutic target**.
- **Side effect (adverse effect)** = the effect of the drug on **other targets**. Sometimes a side effect is **clinically beneficial** (e.g. the **sedative effect** of some **antihistamines**).
- **Toxic effect** = **harmful**, **always unwanted**, must be avoided during therapy.
- **Lethal effect** = **death**.
- **Toxicology** = the science that studies the **harmful effects of drugs**.

<div class="key-points">

**Key points**
- Therapeutic = on the target; side/adverse = on other targets (may be useful, e.g. sedation by antihistamines).
- Toxic = harmful, always unwanted; lethal = death.
- Toxicology = study of harmful drug effects.

</div>

## 4. How to study a drug

For **each drug (or group of drugs)** you should know:
1. **Classification** (drug class)
2. **Mechanism of action**
3. **Pharmacokinetics** (administration, t½, metabolism…)
4. **Pharmacological effects**
5. **Clinical use (indications)**
6. **Adverse effects and toxicity**
7. **Contraindications and precautions**
8. **Drug interactions**
9. **Any specific information related to drug use**

### References
- **Lippincott's Illustrated Reviews: Pharmacology**
- **Katzung's Basic & Clinical Pharmacology**
- **Rang and Dale's Pharmacology**

<div class="key-points">

**Key points**
- Study every drug by: class, mechanism, pharmacokinetics, effects, indications, adverse effects, contraindications, interactions, specifics.
- References: Lippincott, Katzung, Rang & Dale.

## ⚠️ Exam traps
- **Pharmacodynamics = drug → body; pharmacokinetics = body → drug** (most common mix-up).
- **Side effect ≠ toxic effect:** a side effect can be useful (antihistamine sedation); a toxic effect is always unwanted.
- **Drug (final dosage form + excipients) vs medicine (any substance changing biologic function)** — use the lecture's wording.
- **Toxicology** = study of harmful effects.
