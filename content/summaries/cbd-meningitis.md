## 1. Learning objectives

- ⭐ The session links a **meningitis case** to five basic-science objectives:
  1. **Meninges** and the **two layers of the cranial dura mater** (standard teaching: periosteal and meningeal layers).
  2. **Leukocytes**: types, origins, distribution, lifespan, defensive roles.
  3. **Leukocyte migration and phagocytosis** from blood to infected tissue.
  4. **Macrophage distribution** and **splenic red-pulp** structure in host defense.
  5. **Vitamin classification** and **vitamin A** chemistry and immune barriers.
- **Evidence base:** Guyton and Hall Medical Physiology (resistance of the body to infection), Harper's Illustrated Biochemistry, Gray's Anatomy for Students.

## 2. The case and examination

- ⭐ **7-year-old boy**, ER, **2 days** of **high-grade fever, severe headache, repeated vomiting, neck stiffness**; increasingly **drowsy**, refuses to eat.
- **General examination:** **fever 39.8°C**, irritable and drowsy, **tachycardia**, **photophobia**.
- **Neurological examination:** **neck stiffness**, **positive Kernig sign**, **positive Brudzinski sign**, **decreased level of consciousness**.

| Group | Findings |
|---|---|
| General | Fever 39.8°C, irritable/drowsy, tachycardia, photophobia |
| Neurological | Neck stiffness, Kernig +, Brudzinski +, decreased consciousness |

**Key points**
- Fever + headache + vomiting + neck stiffness in a child = suspect meningitis.
- Kernig and Brudzinski signs = meningeal irritation.
- Drowsiness / decreased consciousness is a red flag.

## 3. Investigations

- **Blood:** **CBC: WBC 19,500/µL with neutrophilia**; **CRP ↑**; **ESR ↑**.
- **CSF (lumbar puncture):** **turbid**, **↑ neutrophils**, **↑ protein**, **↓ glucose**, **Gram stain positive for bacteria**.
- **Microbiology:** **blood culture** and **CSF culture**.
- Standard interpretation (not on the slides): this pattern = **bacterial meningitis**; neutrophilia reflects neutrophils being mobilized from blood to infected tissue (objectives 2–3).

**Key points**

## ⚠️ Exam traps

- **Photophobia belongs to the general examination; Kernig and Brudzinski signs belong to the neurological examination.**
- **CSF glucose is DECREASED and protein INCREASED** in this case.
- **Neutrophils** (not lymphocytes) are raised in blood and CSF.
- The slides give **no diagnosis or treatment**: the interpretation "bacterial meningitis" is standard teaching.
