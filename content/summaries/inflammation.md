# Acute Inflammation & Chemical Mediators — مراجعة ليلة الامتحان

هلا بيك 👋 هاي ملزمة الـ **Acute inflammation** — من أحب المواضيع للأساتذة بالأسئلة. راح نمشي من التعريف لحد الـ systemic manifestations. بعد كل موضوع اكو **Key points** و3 أسئلة short essay، اضغط على السؤال حتى يطلع الجواب.

## 1. التعريف والخلايا والأنسجة المشاركة

**Inflammation** هو **protective response** هدفه يتخلص من **السبب الأولي للـ cell injury** ومن **الخلايا والأنسجة الـ necrotic** اللي نتجت عن الأذى. يعني الجسم يدافع ويرتب الفوضى.

منو يشارك بالعملية؟ أربع مجموعات:

1. **Circulating cells**: الـ **leukocytes** (neutrophils، monocytes، eosinophils، lymphocytes، basophils) + **platelets**.
2. **Plasma proteins**: **clotting factors**، **kininogens**، **complement components**.
3. **Connective tissue cells**: **mast cells**، **fibroblasts**، **resident macrophages**، lymphocytes.
4. **Extracellular matrix (ECM)**: يتكون من:
   - **Structural fibrous proteins** (collagen، elastin)
   - **Adhesive glycoproteins** (fibronectin، laminin، nonfibrillar collagen، tenascin)
   - **Proteoglycans**

كلهم يتعاونون حتى يحلّون الأذى الموضعي ويرجعون وظيفة النسيج.

<div dir="ltr" class="key-points">

**Key points**
- Inflammation = protective response to eliminate the initial cause of injury and the necrotic cells/tissue.
- Participants: circulating cells (leukocytes + platelets), plasma proteins (clotting factors, kininogens, complement), connective tissue cells (mast cells, fibroblasts, macrophages, lymphocytes), ECM.
- ECM = fibrous proteins (collagen, elastin) + adhesive glycoproteins (fibronectin, laminin…) + proteoglycans.

</div>

## 2. Etiology و Types و Cardinal signs

### شنو يحفز الالتهاب؟ (6 أسباب)
1. **Infections** (bacterial، viral، parasitic) و **microbial toxins**
2. **Trauma** (blunt و penetrating)
3. **Physical and chemical agents** — thermal injury (**burns**، **frostbite**)، **irradiation**، بعض الـ environmental chemicals
4. **Tissue necrosis** (من أي سبب)
5. **Foreign bodies** (splinters، dirt، sutures)
6. **Immune reactions** (**hypersensitivity reactions**)

### نوعين من الالتهاب

| | Acute | Chronic |
|---|---|---|
| Duration | relatively short | longer |
| Main feature | **fluid and protein exudation** | **vascular proliferation and scarring** |
| Cells | ⭐ **neutrophils** | ⭐ **lymphocytes and macrophages** |

### Cardinal signs
- **Rubor** = redness
- **Tumor** = swelling
- **Calor** = heat
- **Dolor** = pain

هاي الأربعة وصفها **Celsus** بالقرن الأول الميلادي، وبعدين **R. Virchow** ضاف الخامسة: **Functio laesa** = loss of function. هاي العلامات أوضح بالـ acute من الـ chronic.

> ⚠️ **انتبه:** Tumor هنا يعني **swelling** مو ورم! وسؤال "منو ضاف functio laesa؟" جوابه **Virchow** مو Celsus.

<div dir="ltr" class="key-points">

**Key points**
- Triggers: infections/toxins, trauma, physical & chemical agents, tissue necrosis, foreign bodies, immune (hypersensitivity) reactions.
- Acute: short, exudation, neutrophils. Chronic: longer, vascular proliferation + scarring, lymphocytes & macrophages.
- Rubor, tumor, calor, dolor (Celsus); functio laesa added by Virchow.

</div>

**أسئلة Short essay**

**Answer:** Infections (bacterial, viral, parasitic) and microbial toxins; trauma (blunt and penetrating); physical and chemical agents (burns, frostbite, irradiation, environmental chemicals); tissue necrosis; foreign bodies (splinters, dirt, sutures); immune (hypersensitivity) reactions.

**ليش؟** ست نقاط، كل وحدة وياها مثال.
</details>

<details><summary>Q2. Compare acute and chronic inflammation.</summary>

**Answer:** Acute inflammation is of relatively short duration, with fluid and protein exudation and neutrophil accumulation. Chronic inflammation is of longer duration, with vascular proliferation and scarring and an influx of lymphocytes and macrophages.

**ليش؟** المقارنة بثلاث محاور: duration، features، cells.
</details>

<details><summary>Q3. What are the cardinal signs of inflammation and who described them?</summary>

**Answer:** Rubor (redness), tumor (swelling), calor (heat) and dolor (pain) were described by Celsus in the 1st century AD; functio laesa (loss of function) was added by Virchow. They are more prominent in acute than chronic inflammation.

**ليش؟** الأسماء اللاتينية + المعنى + منو وصفها.
</details>

## 3. Acute inflammation — Vascular changes

الـ **acute inflammation** هو الـ **immediate and early response** هدفه يوصل الـ **leukocytes** لمكان الأذى. عنده مكونين: **vascular** و **cellular**.

**Vascular changes** نوعين:
1. **Alterations in vascular caliber** → زيادة الـ blood flow (**vasodilation**)
2. **Structural changes in microvasculature** → تسمح للـ plasma proteins والـ leukocytes تطلع (**increased vascular permeability**)

**Cellular changes**: **emigration of leukocytes**، تجمعهم بمكان الأذى، وتفعيلهم حتى يقضون على السبب.

كل هاي الأحداث ينسقها **local release of chemical mediators**. والـ vascular changes مع تجمع الخلايا يفسرون 3 من الـ cardinal signs: **heat، redness، swelling**. أما **pain** و **loss of function** فيجون من الـ **mediators** والـ **leukocyte-mediated damage**.

### تسلسل التغيرات الوعائية (بالترتيب)
1. **Transient constriction of arterioles** (لحظي)
2. **Vasodilation** — من أول العلامات؛ يبدي بالـ **arterioles** وبعدين **opening of new capillary beds** → **increased blood flow → REDNESS + HEAT**
3. **Increased permeability** → **protein-rich fluid** يطلع للـ extravascular tissue
4. **Stasis** — بسبب طلوع السوائل، الـ RBCs تتركز بالأوعية الصغيرة ويزيد **viscosity** الدم
5. **Leukocyte accumulation** على الـ endothelium (خصوصاً **neutrophils**)، يلزگون، وبعدين يهاجرون

### Increased vascular permeability — الـ **hallmark** of acute inflammation
- **Arteriolar vasodilation** → ↑blood flow → ↑**intravascular hydrostatic pressure** → سوائل تطلع = **transudate**
- **Increased vascular permeability** → يطلع **protein-rich plasma = exudate** → ↓**intravascular osmotic pressure** → سوائل أكثر تطلع → **edema (SWELLING)**

> ⚠️ **انتبه:** أول شي يصير هو **transient vasoconstriction** مو vasodilation. والـ **hallmark** هو **increased vascular permeability**.

<div dir="ltr" class="key-points">

**Key points**
- Acute inflammation = immediate response to deliver leukocytes; vascular + cellular components.
- Sequence: transient arteriolar constriction → vasodilation (redness, heat) → ↑permeability → stasis → leukocyte margination.
- Hallmark = increased vascular permeability → exudate → ↓osmotic pressure → edema (swelling).
- Heat, redness, swelling from vascular changes; pain & loss of function from mediators and leukocyte damage.

</div>

**أسئلة Short essay**

<details><summary>Q1. Describe the sequence of vascular changes in acute inflammation.</summary>

**Answer:** A transient constriction of arterioles, followed by vasodilation (first arterioles, then opening of new capillary beds) causing increased blood flow (redness and heat); then increased microvascular permeability with outpouring of protein-rich fluid; stasis due to fluid loss, concentration of red cells and increased blood viscosity; and finally accumulation of leukocytes (mainly neutrophils) along the endothelium, adhesion and migration.

**ليش؟** الترتيب هو كل الدرجة، لا تبدّل خطوة.
</details>

<details><summary>Q2. Explain how increased vascular permeability leads to edema.</summary>

**Answer:** Arteriolar vasodilation increases blood flow and intravascular hydrostatic pressure, moving fluid out of capillaries (transudate). Increased vascular permeability allows protein-rich plasma (exudate) to escape, lowering intravascular osmotic pressure, which causes further outflow of fluid and edema (swelling).

**ليش؟** السؤال يريد الربط بين الضغط الهيدروستاتيكي والأسموزي.
</details>

<details><summary>Q3. Which cardinal signs are explained by vascular changes, and which by mediators?</summary>

**Answer:** Vascular changes and cell recruitment explain heat (calor), redness (rubor) and swelling (tumor). Pain (dolor) and loss of function result from mediator elaboration and leukocyte-mediated damage.

**ليش؟** تقسيم 3 + 2 نقطة مفضلة بالأسئلة.
</details>

## 4. Mechanisms of increased vascular permeability (6)

1. **Formation of endothelial gaps in venules** — بسبب **endothelial cell contraction** (أشيع ميكانيزم).
2. **Direct endothelial injury** → **endothelial cell necrosis and detachment**. يصير بالـ **necrotizing injuries**، **يبدي فوراً ويبقى فترة طويلة** = **immediate sustained response**. يصيب **كل مستويات الـ microcirculation** (venules، capillaries، arterioles).
3. **Delayed prolonged leakage** — يبدي بعد **2–12 hours**، يدوم ساعات أو أيام، ويصيب **venules + capillaries**. السبب: delayed endothelial damage (يمكن **apoptosis**) أو **cytokines** تسبب **endothelial retraction**.
4. **Leukocyte-mediated endothelial injury** — الـ leukocytes الملزوگة تتفعل وتطلق **toxic oxygen species** و **proteolytic enzymes** تأذي الـ endothelium.
5. **Increased transcytosis** — عبر channels اسمها **vesiculovacuolar organelle** (قريبة من الـ intercellular junctions). **VEGF** يزيد عددها وحجمها.
6. **Leakage from new blood vessels** — بالـ repair تتكون أوعية جديدة (**angiogenesis**)، وتبقى **leaky** لحد ما الـ endothelial cells تنضج وتسوي **intercellular junctions**.

> ⚠️ **انتبه:** **Immediate sustained** = direct injury (all levels). **Delayed prolonged** = 2–12 h (venules + capillaries). لا تخلطهم.

<div dir="ltr" class="key-points">

**Key points**
- 6 mechanisms: endothelial gaps in venules; direct endothelial injury (immediate sustained, all levels); delayed prolonged leakage (2–12 h, venules + capillaries); leukocyte-mediated injury; transcytosis (vesiculovacuolar organelle, VEGF); leaky new vessels (angiogenesis).

</div>

**أسئلة Short essay**

<details><summary>Q1. List the mechanisms of increased vascular permeability in acute inflammation.</summary>

**Answer:** 1) Endothelial gaps in venules (endothelial contraction). 2) Direct endothelial injury (immediate sustained response). 3) Delayed prolonged leakage. 4) Leukocyte-mediated endothelial injury. 5) Increased transcytosis via the vesiculovacuolar organelle (e.g. VEGF). 6) Leakage from new blood vessels during angiogenesis.

**ليش؟** الست نقاط كاملة مع كلمة مفتاحية لكل وحدة.
</details>

<details><summary>Q2. Compare the immediate sustained response and delayed prolonged leakage.</summary>

**Answer:** Immediate sustained response: direct endothelial injury (necrosis and detachment) in necrotizing injuries; starts immediately, lasts long, affects all levels of the microcirculation. Delayed prolonged leakage: begins after 2–12 hours, lasts hours to days, involves venules and capillaries; due to delayed endothelial damage (perhaps apoptosis) or cytokine-induced endothelial retraction.

**ليش؟** مقارنة توقيت + مكان + سبب.
</details>

<details><summary>Q3. What is transcytosis and which factor increases it?</summary>

**Answer:** Transport across the endothelial cytoplasm through channels of interconnected uncoated vesicles and vacuoles called the vesiculovacuolar organelle, located near intercellular junctions. Vascular endothelial growth factor (VEGF) increases the number and perhaps the size of these channels.

**ليش؟** الاسم + VEGF هم مفتاح الجواب.
</details>

## 5. Cellular events — Extravasation & Phagocytosis

وظيفة أساسية للالتهاب هي يوصل الـ **leukocytes** لمكان الأذى ويفعّلهم: يبلعون (**phagocytose**) الأسباب، يقتلون الـ microbes، وينظفون الـ necrotic tissue والأجسام الغريبة.

### Extravasation (طلوع الـ leukocytes من الوعاء للنسيج)
1. **Margination & rolling** — بواسطة **selectins**
2. **Adhesion to endothelium** — بواسطة **integrins** — و **transmigration** عبر الـ endothelium (**diapedesis**)
3. **Migration (chemotaxis)** بالنسيج باتجاه **chemotactic stimulus**
4. **Phagocytosis** (degradation and killing)

### Phagocytosis (3 خطوات)
1. **Recognition and attachment** للجسم المراد بلعه
2. **Engulfment** وتكوين **phagocytic vacuole**
3. **Killing or degradation**

### Killing
- **Oxygen-dependent mechanisms**
- **Non-oxygen-dependent mechanisms**
- بعد الـ phagocytosis، الـ **neutrophils** يسوون **apoptosis** بسرعة وتبلعهم الـ **macrophages**.

> ⚠️ **انتبه:** **Selectin = rolling**، **Integrin = adhesion**. هاي أكثر شي ينسأل بهذا الجزء.

<div dir="ltr" class="key-points">

**Key points**
- Extravasation: margination & rolling (selectins) → adhesion (integrins) & transmigration/diapedesis → chemotaxis → phagocytosis.
- Phagocytosis: recognition & attachment → engulfment (phagocytic vacuole) → killing/degradation.
- Killing: oxygen-dependent and oxygen-independent; neutrophils then die by apoptosis and are eaten by macrophages.

</div>

**أسئلة Short essay**

<details><summary>Q1. Describe the steps of leukocyte extravasation.</summary>

**Answer:** 1) Margination and rolling (mediated by selectins). 2) Adhesion to the endothelium (integrins) and transmigration across it (diapedesis). 3) Migration in the interstitial tissue toward a chemotactic stimulus (chemotaxis). 4) Phagocytosis, with killing and degradation.

**ليش؟** الترتيب + الـ molecule لكل خطوة.
</details>

<details><summary>Q2. What are the steps of phagocytosis?</summary>

**Answer:** Recognition and attachment of the particle to the leukocyte; engulfment with formation of a phagocytic vacuole; and killing or degradation of the ingested material.

**ليش؟** ثلاث خطوات مرتبة.
</details>

<details><summary>Q3. What happens to neutrophils after phagocytosis?</summary>

**Answer:** They rapidly undergo apoptotic cell death and are ingested by macrophages.

**ليش؟** نقطة صغيرة بس تجي كسؤال مباشر.
</details>

## 6. Morphologic patterns & Outcomes & Definitions

### Morphologic patterns
1. **Serous inflammation** — **outpouring of thin fluid** (من الـ plasma أو الـ mesothelium) → **effusion**؛ مثال: **skin blister**.
2. **Fibrinous inflammation** — بالإصابات الأشد؛ الـ **fibrinogen** يعبر وتتكون **fibrin** خارج الأوعية. يصير إذا الـ leak كبير أو اكو **procoagulant stimulus** بالنسيج (مثل **cancer cells**).
3. **Suppurative (purulent)** — إنتاج كميات كبيرة من **pus** = neutrophils + necrotic cells + edema fluid.
   - **Abscess** = **localized collection of purulent tissue** مدفونة بنسيج أو عضو أو مساحة محصورة؛ سببه **deep seeding of pyogenic bacteria** (مثل **Staphylococcus**).
4. **Ulcer** — **discontinuation of epithelium** يسبب حفرة على سطح عضو، بسبب **sloughing of inflammatory necrotic tissue**.

### Outcomes of acute inflammation
**Resolution**، **abscess formation**، **healing by fibrosis**، أو التحول لـ **chronic inflammation**.

### تعاريف لازم تحفظها حرفياً
| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| **Exudation** | escape of fluid، proteins، blood cells من الأوعية للـ interstitium أو الـ body cavities |
| **Exudate** | inflammatory fluid، **high protein**، cellular debris، **specific gravity > 1.020** → يعني تغير بالـ permeability |
| **Transudate** | **low protein** (mostly **albumin**)، **specific gravity < 1.012**؛ ultrafiltrate بسبب **osmotic/hydrostatic imbalance** **بدون** زيادة permeability |
| **Edema** | excess fluid بالـ interstitium أو الـ serous cavities؛ ممكن **exudate أو transudate** |
| **Pus** | purulent exudate غني بالـ **leukocytes (mostly neutrophils)**، debris، وأحياناً microbes |

> ⚠️ **انتبه:** الأرقام: exudate **>1.020**، transudate **<1.012**. والـ edema ممكن يكون الاثنين.

<div dir="ltr" class="key-points">

**Key points**
- Patterns: serous (thin fluid, blister), fibrinous (fibrin; large leaks or procoagulant stimulus), suppurative/purulent (pus; abscess by pyogenic bacteria e.g. Staphylococcus), ulcer (surface defect from sloughing necrotic tissue).
- Outcomes: resolution, abscess, fibrosis, chronic inflammation.
- Exudate: high protein, SG > 1.020, ↑permeability. Transudate: low protein (albumin), SG < 1.012, no ↑permeability.
- Edema = exudate or transudate; pus = purulent exudate rich in neutrophils.

</div>

**أسئلة Short essay**

<details><summary>Q1. Differentiate between exudate and transudate.</summary>

**Answer:** Exudate is an inflammatory extravascular fluid with high protein concentration, cellular debris and specific gravity above 1.020, implying increased vascular permeability. Transudate has low protein content (mostly albumin) and specific gravity below 1.012; it is an ultrafiltrate of plasma resulting from osmotic or hydrostatic imbalance without increased permeability.

**ليش؟** من أشهر أسئلة الـ short essay، لازم الأرقام.
</details>

<details><summary>Q2. Describe the morphologic patterns of acute inflammation.</summary>

**Answer:** Serous (thin fluid effusion, e.g. skin blister); fibrinous (fibrin deposition when leaks are large or with a procoagulant stimulus, e.g. cancer cells); suppurative/purulent (pus of neutrophils, necrotic cells and edema fluid; abscess = localized collection due to pyogenic bacteria such as Staphylococcus); ulcer (local surface defect due to sloughing of inflamed necrotic tissue).

**ليش؟** أربع أنماط مع مثال لكل واحد.
</details>

<details><summary>Q3. Define abscess and list the outcomes of acute inflammation.</summary>

**Answer:** An abscess is a localized collection of purulent inflammatory tissue buried in a tissue, organ or confined space, produced by deep seeding of pyogenic bacteria. Outcomes of acute inflammation: resolution, abscess formation, healing by fibrosis, or progression to chronic inflammation.

**ليش؟** التعريف + الأربع outcomes.
</details>

## 7. Chemical mediators

**Definition:** أي **messenger** يأثر على الأوعية، الخلايا الالتهابية، أو خلايا ثانية ويساهم بالـ inflammatory response.

### Classification
- **Plasma-derived**: complement، kinins، coagulation factors — أغلبها بشكل **"pro-form"** يحتاج **activation (enzymatic cleavage)**.
- **Cell-derived**:
  - **Preformed and sequestered** وينطلق — مثل **histamine** بالـ **mast cells**
  - **Synthesized as needed** — مثل **prostaglandin**

### Characteristics
- ممكن تستخدم **specific receptor** أو لا.
- ممكن تخلي الخلايا تطلق mediators ثانية تزيد أو تقلل الاستجابة (**regulation**).
- **Tightly regulated**: تتحلل بسرعة (AA metabolites)، تتعطل إنزيمياً (**kininase**)، أو تنشال (**antioxidants**).
- **Mechanism**: **receptor-ligand interactions** (الأساسي)، **direct enzymatic activity**، **oxidative damage**.
- شبكة معقدة مع **redundancy** عالية → تضمن التضخيم والاستمرار. **Short t½** وممكن تكون **harmful**.

### Types
- **Vasoactive amines**: **histamine**، **serotonin**
- **Plasma proteins**: complement، kinin، coagulation factors
- **Arachidonic acid metabolites**: **prostaglandins**، **leukotrienes**، **lipoxins**
- **Platelet activating factor (PAF)**
- **Nitric oxide (NO)**
- **Chemokines and cytokines**

### ⭐ Role of mediators (أهم جدول بالملزمة)
| Reaction | Mediators |
|---|---|
| **Vasodilation** | **PGs، NO، histamine** |
| **Increased permeability** | vasoactive amines، **anaphylatoxins**، **bradykinin**، **LTs**، **PAF**، **substance P** |
| **Leukocyte events** (chemotaxis/activation) | **C5a**، **LTB4**، chemokines، **IL-1**، **TNF**، bacterial products |
| **Fever** | **IL-1، TNF، PGs** |
| **Pain** | **PGs، bradykinin** |
| **Tissue damage** | lysosomal enzymes، O₂ metabolites، NO |

> ⚠️ **انتبه:** الـ **PGs** موجودة بثلاث خانات: vasodilation، fever، pain. والـ **Pain = PGs + bradykinin** فقط. **Fever = IL-1 + TNF + PGs**.

<div dir="ltr" class="key-points">

**Key points**
- Plasma-derived (pro-forms needing cleavage) vs cell-derived (preformed: histamine; synthesized: PGs).
- Tightly regulated: decay, enzymatic inactivation (kininase), scavenging (antioxidants); short t½, redundant, can be harmful.
- Vasodilation: PGs, NO, histamine. Permeability: amines, anaphylatoxins, bradykinin, LTs, PAF, substance P.
- Chemotaxis: C5a, LTB4, chemokines, IL-1, TNF. Fever: IL-1, TNF, PGs. Pain: PGs, bradykinin.

</div>

**أسئلة Short essay**

<details><summary>Q1. Classify chemical mediators of inflammation with examples.</summary>

**Answer:** Plasma-derived: complement, kinins, coagulation factors — mostly circulating as pro-forms that require enzymatic activation. Cell-derived: preformed and sequestered (e.g. mast cell histamine) or synthesized as needed (e.g. prostaglandins). Types include vasoactive amines, plasma proteins, arachidonic acid metabolites (PGs, LTs, lipoxins), PAF, nitric oxide, chemokines and cytokines.

**ليش؟** التصنيف الرئيسي + الأمثلة.
</details>

<details><summary>Q2. Name the mediators responsible for vasodilation, fever and pain.</summary>

**Answer:** Vasodilation: prostaglandins, nitric oxide, histamine. Fever: IL-1, TNF, prostaglandins. Pain: prostaglandins, bradykinin.

**ليش؟** جدول الأدوار هو الأكثر سؤالاً.
</details>

<details><summary>Q3. How are chemical mediators regulated?</summary>

**Answer:** They are tightly regulated: they quickly decay (arachidonic acid metabolites), are inactivated enzymatically (kininase) or are scavenged (antioxidants). They may also signal cells to release other molecules that amplify or inhibit the response; they have short half-lives and are potentially harmful.

**ليش؟** الطرق الثلاث للتنظيم بأمثلتها.
</details>

## 8. Systemic manifestations (SIRS)

الالتهاب مو بس موضعي، ممكن يسوي **systemic inflammatory response syndrome (SIRS)**:
1. **Fever**
2. **Acute phase proteins**
3. **Leukocytosis**
4. **Other manifestations of the acute phase response**
5. بالـ **severe bacterial infections (sepsis)**: كميات كبيرة من الـ organisms والـ **LPS** بالدم تحفز إنتاج **كميات هائلة من الـ cytokines**.

<div dir="ltr" class="key-points">

**Key points**
- SIRS: fever, acute phase proteins, leukocytosis, other acute phase responses.
- Sepsis: organisms + LPS in blood → enormous cytokine production.

</div>

**أسئلة Short essay**

<details><summary>Q1. List the systemic manifestations of inflammation.</summary>

**Answer:** Fever, acute phase proteins, leukocytosis and other manifestations of the acute phase response; in severe bacterial infection (sepsis), large amounts of organisms and LPS in the blood stimulate production of enormous quantities of cytokines.

**ليش؟** قائمة الـ SIRS مباشرة.
</details>

<details><summary>Q2. What is the role of LPS in sepsis?</summary>

**Answer:** In severe bacterial infections, large amounts of organisms and LPS in the blood stimulate the production of enormous quantities of several cytokines, producing a severe systemic response.

**ليش؟** الربط بين LPS والـ cytokines.
</details>

<details><summary>Q3. Which mediators produce fever, and what is SIRS?</summary>

**Answer:** Fever is produced by IL-1, TNF and prostaglandins. SIRS (systemic inflammatory response syndrome) is the systemic manifestation of inflammation: fever, acute phase proteins, leukocytosis and other acute phase responses.

**ليش؟** يربط جدول الـ mediators بالـ SIRS.
</details>

## 🔥 الخلاصة النهائية — احفظها قبل الامتحان

1. **Inflammation** = protective response يشيل السبب والـ necrotic tissue.
2. Participants: **leukocytes + platelets**، **plasma proteins** (clotting، kininogens، complement)، **CT cells** (mast cells، fibroblasts، macrophages)، **ECM**.
3. Triggers: infection، trauma، physical/chemical، **necrosis**، foreign bodies، **hypersensitivity**.
4. **Acute** = short، exudation، **neutrophils** | **Chronic** = long، scarring، **lymphocytes + macrophages**.
5. **Rubor، tumor، calor، dolor** (Celsus) + **functio laesa** (Virchow).
6. Sequence: **transient vasoconstriction → vasodilation → ↑permeability → stasis → margination**.
7. **Hallmark = increased vascular permeability**؛ exudate → ↓osmotic pressure → **edema**.
8. Permeability mechanisms: venular gaps، direct injury (**immediate sustained**)، **delayed prolonged (2–12 h)**، leukocyte-mediated، **transcytosis (VEGF)**، new leaky vessels.
9. Extravasation: **rolling (selectins) → adhesion (integrins) → diapedesis → chemotaxis → phagocytosis**.
10. Phagocytosis: **recognition → engulfment → killing**؛ neutrophils die by **apoptosis**.
11. Patterns: **serous (blister)**، **fibrinous**، **purulent (pus، abscess، Staph)**، **ulcer**.
12. **Exudate SG >1.020 (high protein)** vs **Transudate SG <1.012 (albumin)**.
13. Mediators: **vasodilation = PGs، NO، histamine**؛ **fever = IL-1، TNF، PGs**؛ **pain = PGs، bradykinin**؛ **chemotaxis = C5a، LTB4، IL-1، TNF**.
14. **SIRS**: fever، acute phase proteins، leukocytosis؛ **sepsis → LPS → cytokine storm**.

بالتوفيق 💪 ركّز على جدول الـ mediators والفرق بين exudate و transudate.
