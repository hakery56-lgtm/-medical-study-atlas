  (kidneys: FMA7204 right kidney, FMA7205 left kidney)

Changes: exported one file per body system, labels and helper objects removed,
vessels/nerves converted from curves to meshes, meshes simplified and
meshopt-compressed. Z-Anatomy's inner ear (Univ. of Dundee, CC-BY-NC-SA 4.0) and
kidney (L. Cowley, CC-BY-NC 4.0) models are NOT included; the kidneys were
replaced with BodyParts3D meshes.

Regenerate with scripts/export-anatomy.py (see its header).
